# Recipe: «Ротунда» (Rotunda Libros) — original rotunda on the skeleton + broad-nib engine

Source: `core25.js` (one IIFE, loaded last in `assemble.py`). Registry pattern = Liniya (`core18a.js`): `F={id,name,family,S0,GL}`, `F.pen`, `F.kit`, `skDef` glyphs, `skPunct`, `FONTS[id]=F`; plus `KERN_TABLE`, `WSCALE`, `famParams` wrapper, `CU.J/CAP` (custom-glyph pens).
1. Order (type-design-foundation): metrics + pen (capH 660, xh 490, asc 720, desc 215, stem 168 / hair 30, nib 36°, wdth 96) -> control glyphs o n d (+ b a e) -> families (bowls = circles via `K.ov_`, arches, versal rounds C D E G O Q U, straights) -> Cyrillic from the same builders -> digits, punctuation -> spacing (sb 36) -> kerning (autoclass `KERN_TABLE_BASE`) -> ss01 -> QA.
2. Broad nib = `skPenOf({kind:'ell'})`: rx=stem/2, ry=hair/2, angle `nib`. Contrast comes only from the pen: stem width = hypot(S cos a, H sin a), horizontals = hypot(S sin a, H cos a); counters become lancets by themselves. Keep `hair` 0.14-0.36 of stem and `nib` 28-38 (`F.lim`, `nibX` bounds vertical pen extent <= 0.27 xh).
3. Cap rules (the key to a clean broad-nib look): never cut a stroke with a line (nearly) parallel to it, that makes a long sliver. Steep stems/hooks: horizontal cut `'H'`. Upper arc terminals (c e C G S s З): nib-edge cut `K.nb={cut:[cos a,sin a]}`. Lower/horizontal-ish ends: vertical cut `'V'`. Never `'V'` on a near-vertical end (use `'H'`).
4. Stem-cut style = font param `term` (0 round, 1 flat, 2 chisel along the nib edge, 3 half chisel = default; 2 is correct but its cut rise (stem*tan nib) trips the stem probe at Black). Implemented once by `stemCuts()` in `d()`: it rewrites `'H'` caps on steep stroke ends to `K.stc` and insets the end by half-width*tan(cut angle) so the cut corner sits on baseline/x-line/cap-line (no overshoot); glyph code stays unchanged.
5. Diamond i-dot / punctuation dots: `K.dia` = short stroke across the nib with flat caps (a parallelogram, like a pen touch). Marks for Й Ё are own `SKM.rbreve/rdier` (global marks untouched).
6. Topology safety (stress): counters must stay open at any slider value. Use adaptive geometry (a: bowl top and hook height follow pen thickness 2·hy), `xAtY()` so a second stroke enters a curved first stroke exactly (y), explicit start tangents (2, 3, З), and tight `F.lim` (stemK 0.41, hairR, hairX, nibX, wdth >= 88, ascX).
7. Weights: `famParams` wrapper multiplies pen width (Light .72, Bold 1.07, Black 1.14 of Regular; stem max 192, heavier closes f/&/9) and hair by k^0.6, wdth +5% per 100% growth; `WSCALE.rotunda=0.83` keeps names/`famNearest` consistent.
8. Italic: `slant 10`, wdth 90, nib 32 (slanted rotunda, no separate forms). ss01 = 2-shaped `r.ss01` and long `s.ss01` (`ss01Name` is set), made like `m.ss01` in core18z; `otAddAlternates(G,false)` gives case/tnum/pnum, its `m.ss01` is deleted.
9. `F.charset='basic'` declared; only a–z A–Z А–Я Ёё 0–9 and `.,:;!?«»"'-–—()/&№` + space/nbsp are kept in GL/CHARSET (others created by `skPunct` are deleted).
10. Proof: `F.proof={stem:p=>hypot(S cos a,H sin a), noBar:true, stemSet}` (pen-aware stem, no bar check). Tests added to `check.sh`: t10 lane + md5 (12), homo (6 lines), `t12p rotunda` (8 x 0), slow: fam md5 48, `stress-rotunda` (150 x seeds 1,2 + pres).
11. Redesign (lead review: 'reads as flared sans'): colour first. Stem 168 on xh 490 (nib width ~0.17 em), hair 30 (contrast >5:1), wdth 96, short ascenders (asc/xh 1.47), desc 215; `a` = round bowl + stem, `d` = plain bowl + stem (Polanco rows 1), g/j tails cut along the nib, own parens and a 3-stroke `&` (top oval + open lower arc + diagonal through the arc end), f hook ends 'V' at 84 deg (nib cut gave a hairline crack). Limits retuned by stress: wdth 92-108, nib 28-38, capH >= 630, ascX 1.35, descX 1.2.
12. QA round F2 (user: letters misread): М/м (shared) = straight stems + wedge to baseline (round shoulders read as «ᗰ»); parens = rounder arc, ends inset 0.42 w (wider overflowed proof margin); « » = big chevrons (small ones became diamonds under a 168 pen); Ж ж Ш Щ ш щ +40 width, sb 36 -> 42 (gaps ~85 u).
13. Tried and rejected (pen already makes lancet counters, do not repeat): two-arc lancet o (eye-shaped slit counters, 7-18 deg tips); diagonal foot strokes on i n (spiky/descend under half-chisel `stemCuts`). A real rotunda foot needs a dedicated terminal primitive in `stemCuts`, not extra strokes.
14. Round 2 (lead review): Е/E = stem + three bars (round form read as Є; Ё/Е shared); Й breve = small nib arc cup (`rbreve`, wedge read as accent); & = open upper bowl + open lower arc + leg (closed bowls read as 8); ? = clean hook ending above the dot (`ye`); 1 flag lower/flatter (nib makes / thin); № = N + ring 0.22 h + underline (small ring fills); Ж arms end at stem edge (xs = cx + 0.34 S).
15. Open: Ж/ж still heavy at Black, hairline right leg of Ж follows the thin nib direction; ss01 round r untouched. Judge letters against a serif bold side by side: `qa_F2/cmp.py`.
16. W88 A: `@` (core25.js d('@')) is adaptive: ring radius, inner a pen scale (node w) and the ring's second control y are derived from sampled clearance (24 ellipse points + stem top) of the a to the ring inner; do not shrink ri via the sweep (the a collapsed to 1/0). Stress40 bad 0; seed 7 / 150 still shows `6` and `c` (not addressed).

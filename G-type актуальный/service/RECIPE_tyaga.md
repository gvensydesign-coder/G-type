# Recipe: «Тяга» (id `tyaga`) — stretch monoline caps. core_tyaga.js = tyaga/{engine,tail}.js (mk_tyaga.sh); UI: ui_tyaga.js (tool «Блок», «~» in the sample); tests: tests/e2e_tyaga.py (lanes tyaga, tyaga-mech, tyaga-block); proof: t12p.py tyaga

Idea: geometric minimalist monoline, CAPS ONLY (lower case codepoints draw the same letters). Only STRAIGHT HORIZONTAL segments elongate; stems, round ends, bowls and counters never change. A letter can be stretched in the font itself («~» after it), in the sample block with justification («Блок») and by hand (drag the right edge).
User-approved prototype (w82/S1: RECIPE draft, tyaga.js, proto.html, build_ttf.py); round 2 defaults = the reference poster «ПЛЕ / СНЕ / ВЫЙ / СЫР». This recipe is the product integration; glyph logic is the prototype's, unchanged.

## Measured / construction (from the prototype, units UPM 1000, cap 700, asc 950, desc −260; stroke S = 0.1875 H = 131.25 u)
- Round strokes S2 = S × 1.08 (С О Э Ю Q 0), letter gap 0.135 H (sidebearing 0.0675 H per side), word space 0.36 H + gap, line gap 0.135 H. Base letters Н П Л Е И В Й Ы Р = 1.0 H square; В Р poke +0.02 H (advance counts the nominal width). В: bars at top/mid/bottom, bowl = half-ring, centre-line radius (H−S)/4, outer 0.29 H; Р = upper bowl, Ь/Ы = lower bowl. С: left half-ellipse rx 0.465 H, overshoot 0.0104 H, ink starts 0.028 H inside the flat edge. Diagonals: horizontal width = S / cos θ (clamp 2.2 S), the perpendicular stroke stays S. Й: S×S block at the cap line, no accent. Ё: dots 0.72 S, clearance 0.4 S. Д: П-body + base bar + two square descenders (0.24 H). Ф: stem + two half-ring bowls (0.74 H) joined by bars. З: waist from 0.4 H, two bowls. 6/9: lower stadium + curved arm.
- Shared forms are ONE glyph: А В Е К М Н О Р С Т Х У(=Y) І(=I) and their lower case. Lower case Latin/Cyrillic = the capital (same GL entry, `homo.js` passes).
- Charset (basic): А–Я Ё, A–Z, a–z, а–я ё (same drawings), 0–9, `. , : ; ! ? « » „ “ ” ‘ ’ … " ' - – — − ( ) / & № + = % @ # * © ₽ ° • · \ |`, spaces (U+0020, U+00A0, U+2009, U+202F) and the service glyph `~`. `F._basicDone=true` (the common basic trim would cut `~`). Symbols @ # * © ₽ ° • · \ | were added in v83 (not in the prototype, see «Symbols» below; the cover lane no longer lists them as «нет»).

## Stretch model (class per glyph; k = width multiplier)
- FREE (k 1…6; font steps ×1.5 ×2 ×3 ×4 ×6 = `.s1…s5`): Е Ё F Г L Н П Т Ш Щ Ц Ч Д, В Р Ь Б Ъ З, Ы, С О Э Ю Ф D G Q S U J 0 8 6 9 5, - – — − = +.
- DIAGONAL (k 1…2, steps ×1.5 ×2 = `.s1 .s2`, then lock): А Л И Й К Ж М Х У(Y) Я N V W Z R 2 3 4 7.
- HORIZONTAL (class `h`, steps ×1.5 ×2 = `.s1 .s2`, then lock; free like the first group but capped at ×2): ₽ #.
- NONE: @ * © ° • · \ | and І(I) . , : ; ! ? « » „ “ ” ‘ ’ " ' … ( ) / & № % 1 ( ) — and `~` itself.
- Justify (block): per line extra = block width − natural. «На одну букву» fills the best candidate up to its cap, then the next (scores С 10, Е Э Ы 9, Ь Ъ З О 8, Б Г Т 7 …, diagonals 1); «Равномерно» water-fills FREE letters weighted by score × width. Typed `~` letters and dragged letters are locked. Nothing stretchable → the slack becomes tracking.

## Product integration (follow the «Нить» pattern)
1. engine.js = tyaga.js (generator, layout, justify) without the canvas export; contours are line/arc segments. tail.js converts them to host nodes (`tyNodes`: arcs cut at 90°, cubic handles 4/3·tan(Δ/4)), `GL` entries return the RAW overlapping contours; `finalGlyph` does the host union (`csgUnion`) — also for export. Glyph = `tyGet(ch, p, k)` cached by (glyph, stroke, round, base, apr, k).
2. Registry (`FONTS.tyaga`): GL (lower case and Latin twins share the entry of the canonical glyph), `~` zero width (`finalGlyph` wrapper: the host minimum glyph width of 40 u is cancelled), spaces, CHARSET/GROUPS, DEFAULTS regular + italic (italic = host shear, slant 10), `F.params` (own sliders only; every other host slider is hidden: `HID`), `paramLimits`/`tyLim` (safe ranges), `famParams`, `WSCALE.tyaga = 0.5952` (default stem/xh 0.1875 reads Regular), `KERN_TABLE.tyaga=[]`, `isTyTilde` (the status/proof loops skip the service glyph like spaces).
3. Alternates: `uniXXXX.sN` GL entries (`opt.alt, own, kernAs, tyAlt`) named after the CANONICAL glyph (С = uni0043, Cyrillic С and Latin C and c, с share it). Free letters 5, diagonal 2 → 5×… alternates; every file carries them.
4. GSUB (`otFeaturesO1` hook, as Сцепка): ONE type-4 ligature lookup `letter ~ … ~ → letter.sN` (longest first) registered in `calt`, `liga`, `clig` — Figma applies calt, Word liga/calt. Rules exist for every codepoint of the glyph (А A а a …) → same alternate. Extra tildes after the last step are invisible (zero width).
5. GPOS kern: `classKernTable` override: classes by canonical glyph (members: all codepoints), values from `TYG.KERN` (H units × 700): Г Т F before А Д Л −0.07; А before Т У V W −0.07; Л before Т У V W Г −0.07; Д before Т У V W −0.04; Г А Л Т У V W F before . , … −0.08; У V W Т before О С Q G −0.025. Alternates join the class of their base (`kernAs`) — `Г~А` kerns like `ГА`.
6. Preview: `ui_tyaga.js` replaces `letter + ~×n` by the alternate (private-use symbol via `OTU.ch`, as `ui_stsepka.js`) in `layout`/`ttLayout`; `classKernValue` maps those symbols back to the base. The sample, tester and kerning tab show the font mechanism.
7. Hygiene: `HYG.tyaga = {snap:0,minSeg:0,smooth:null,blunt:0}` (exact geometry); PANOSE `{kind:2,serif:11,arm:2,form:'square',cls:[8,0]}`; `FX.tyaga` (kernMax only); library: `FONT_META.tyaga` (2026; tags geo nocon disp exp basic cl), `LIB_POP`, `LIB_NEW`, collection «Экспериментальные» (`ui_podb.js`); `PDESC`/`EXPL` for the six keys (`ui_b.js`, `ui_expl.js`); `check.sh` (proof-tyaga, deco-geom, counts from the registry: `fontcount.js`), `t10par.sh`, `golden.md5` (lib-tyaga-*, fam/Tyaga-*).

## Symbols (v83: @ # * © ₽ ° • · \ |)
Same logic as the letters: stroke S (the ring/inner parts use 0.62–0.8 S so the small forms keep counters), butt ends, half-ring rounds (`cs`, `stad`) so «Скругление» applies (0 = square corners with the same counters), no sliders added. New helpers in `kit`: `blob` (rounded rectangle, one contour; • disc at 100 %), `rbar` (rotated bar, for *).
- `₽` = Р (stem shifted right by 0.14 H, bowl as Р) + a horizontal bar at 0.22 H that crosses the stem and ends at 78 % of the width; width 1.1 H. `#` = two stems (inset 0.16 H from both ends) + two bars at ±0.21 H around the cap middle, width 1.0 H, min width 2·0.16 H + 3 S (stems never merge). Both class `h`: the bars lengthen, stems/bowls/bar spacing do not.
- `@` = С-ring (arms to the right) + inner a: pill ring 0.44 H high + a stem on its right foot that fuses into the lower arm; width 0.97 H, min 1.35 S + 0.64 H. `©` = ring (stad, 0.62 S, diameter H + overshoot) with a small С (0.42 × 0.5 H) in the middle. `°` ring 0.44 H diameter at the cap top (0.7 S). `•` disc 0.3 H centred on the cap middle (square at Скругление 0, like the period). `·` S×S square on the cap middle (the period's twin). `*` three bars (90°, ±30°), length max(0.46 H, 2.6 S), top at cap height. `\` = mirror of `/`. `|` stem from −0.24 H to the cap line.
- Kern (H units): А Л Y V W before ° * −0.06; ° * before А Л Y V W −0.04. Classes: members of every codepoint as for letters.
- Stretch: ₽ and # have `.s1 .s2` alternates (uni20BD, uni0023); `«₽~»` ×1.5, `«₽~~»` ×2 (third tilde invisible). The others ignore «~».
- QA: e2e_tyaga asserts presence, ink inside the cell, classes, ₽/# alternates and widths in TTF/WOFF/family (HarfBuzz), @ * … do not stretch; `stress_tyaga.js ext | 60 1 | 60 2` keeps the default topology of every symbol and alternate.

## Parameters (own prefixed keys; safe ranges from the prototype qa.py: 11 extreme sets × all glyphs × k = 1, 1.5, kmax → topology identical to the default)
| key | label | range | default | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tsw` | Толщина, % | 12–23 (step 0.25) | 18.75 | the ONLY stroke width (host `stem` derived: tsw × 7 u) |
| `trd` | Скругление, % | 0–100 (5) | 100 | 0 = square bowls with the same counters |
| `tbs` | Базовая ширина, % | 80–130 (1) | 100 | per-glyph minimum widths enforced |
| `tap` | Апрош, % | 4–30 (0.5) | 13.5 | sidebearing sum; host `space` derived (0.36 H + apr) |
| `tst` | Растяжка, × | 1–6 (0.05) | 1 | default factor: plain letter = min(class max, tst); `.sN` = min(class max, step × tst); the block ignores it when justified |
| `tlg` | Межстрочный (блок), % | 6–40 (0.5) | 13.5 | listed in the panel ONLY while the block is shown (`F.params.tlg` switched in `buildPanel`) |

## Tool «Блок» (ui_tyaga.js; CSS block `== T2 ==`)
- Toggle «Образец | Блок» in the header row of the sample pane (visible only when Тяга is open; first visit opens the pane once). Desktop ≥ 1061: the pane takes the whole editor area (glyph list/stage hidden), canvas left, controls right (settings drawer shifts it); ≤ 1060: stacked. Canvas stays the «paper» (`--canvas`) with `--glyph` ink in both themes; marks are `--ink` (accent red only on «Скачать PNG»).
- Controls: multi-line text (≤ 12 lines × 400 chars), «Ширина блока» 2–16 H (default 5.55 H = the poster), toggle «Выровнять блок», segmented «На одну букву» (default) / «Равномерно», «Сбросить растяжку», «Скопировать текст с ~», «SVG», «Скачать PNG».
- Manual stretch: drag the right edge of a letter (hit 12 px, 22 px on touch; `touch-action: pan-y`); keyboard: canvas is focusable, ↑↓ choose a letter, ←→ stretch by 0.1 (Shift 0.5), Delete/Home reset; status line (`role=status`) names the letter, class and factor. Diagonal letters lock at ×2, free at ×6, others have no handle.
- Output: PNG (≤ 2400 px wide) and SVG (outlines after `csgUnion`, one path per letter) via `saveFile` (download capability or anchor); «Скопировать текст с ~» gives the typed text where each stretched letter carries the nearest ladder step (`С~~~`), same stretch in Figma/Word with the font (Word: Ligatures on).
- State `S.tyb {on,seen,txt,w,just,mode,man{'line:index':[k,char]}}` lives in the font slot (`S.lib.tyaga.tyb`: wrappers of `stash/mkSlot/activate`), validated on load (`tbClean`); persisted by the usual localStorage writer and project file.

## QA / evidence
- Parity vs prototype: `tests/e2e_tyaga.py` (fixture `tests/fixtures/tyaga_proto.js` = prototype tyaga.js) XOR of ink at 200 px for every glyph and every alternate ≤ 3 %; advances ≤ 1 u. Outline topology (outer/holes) identical to the prototype for all 400+ glyphs and alternates (node check at integration).
- Mechanism: TTF, WOFF and every family file: calt/liga/clig each alone give the alternate widths of the engine in Chromium/HarfBuzz; features off → the tilde is invisible and zero width; kern incl. alternates; italic file the same.
- Proof: `t12p.py tyaga` 0 issues in 4 weights × 2 styles. Host stress (`stress_tyaga.js ext | 60 1 | 60 2`, check.sh slow `stress-tyaga`): all 32 extreme corners and 120 random mixes × every glyph and alternate (405) keep the default topology (outer/hole counts), no NaN, no contour < 0.035 S²: 0 bad, NO exemptions (6/9 round-0 arm bug fixed, % adaptive).
- Decorative styles (`deco-geom`, 7 variants × 411 glyphs, tests/deco_check.py): `shadow_hatch` uses a 2× coarser stripe pitch for tyaga (`core_deco_d1.js`), otherwise the alternates up to ×6 wide gave 9.2× the plain node count (limit 7×); build ~100 s.
- Known / decisions: `& № % ? Q J 2 3` were redrawn in W84 T4 (see «W84 T4 redraw»); Word needs «Standard ligatures»; no diacritics beyond Ё/Й, no Ukrainian/extended letters; @ # * ₽ © ° • · \ | are first drafts (no reference).

## W84 T4 redraw, kerning, share, hint
- Redrawn in the face's own logic (stroke S, butt ends, half-ring rounds, square dots, Скругление applies, no new sliders). `&` = upper loop (hU = max(0.56 H, 2.6 S), roughly square) over a wider lower lobe sharing one bar, diagonal leg from inside that bar to the right foot, bottom bar hidden inside the leg (distinct from 8: no right wall, leg). `№` = N + open `o` ring (0.66 S, counter always visible: size max(0.5 H, 3.3 t)) + underline. `%` = two rings (0.64 S) + slash; width ADAPTS (fixed point on the slash width) so the rings keep a clear gap g = max(0.3 S, 36 u) from the slash at every stroke/round: topology always 3 outer / 2 holes. `?` hook = top bar + half-ring + stem, stem bottom ≥ 1.75 S above the baseline (gap to the square dot). `Q` tail: diagonal starting inside the bottom wall (top at 0.7 S, below the counter) and leaving 0.2 H below the baseline, right-anchored. `J` hook: left arm to 0.42 H, bottom radius min(0.4 H, W/2). 2 and 3 unchanged in structure (3 flat top + diagonal vs round-topped З). 6/9: at round 0 the arm path now always runs to the cap line (the old code skipped the vertical segment: arm detached, counter lost).
- `! « » ( ) / " '` audited: consistent (stroke S, square at round 0), unchanged.
- Kerning (engine `T.KERN`, helper `KU(left,right,u)` in font units): AV AT AY AW, ЛТ…, LT LY LV LW, ТА ГА УА VA WA YA, PA, Д pairs, Ту, digits 11 17 74, punctuation/dashes/colons/« » after Т Г У V W Y F and quotes/dashes before them and before А Л. 226 class pairs, 21 distinct values 14…110 u (≤ 15 % UPM), max share of one value 13 % (no saturation). `classKernTable` then clamps every pair by the REAL nearest gap of every member glyph and every stretched alternate at the current parameters (G0 = 0.42 × Апрош, 12…60 u; `ct.tU/tL` set so the host guard agrees), so no collision at any slider value. Check: `kern_tyaga.js` (check.sh `kern-tyaga`, 32 corners + random). The block canvas (`TYG.layoutLine`) now applies the same table (it only counted it in `lineInk` before: lines were wider than the justified width).
- Share (`ui_share.js`): when `TYB.poster.on()` (Тяга + «Блок» open) the poster hero is the justified block drawn with engine contours (`ui_tyaga.js` `TYB.poster`: stroke follows the poster weight axis via `famParams` Light…Black, block width constant at every weight, justify mode / manual stretch of the editor). «Знаки» = text of the block, «Своя фраза» = the phrase split in 1…3 lines (5 on 9:16) by the largest fit. Dialog shows a one-line note (`.pst-blk`), `#pst[data-block=1]`. Other fonts and Тяга outside block mode: unchanged (name line). e2e: block poster rows justified (right edges ≤ 4 units apart), inside the gutter, all three formats, phrase, off again = one name line.
- Hint under «Скопировать текст с ~» (`#tyb-cphint`, class in `/* == T4 == */`): Word «Стандартные лигатуры» (Шрифт → Дополнительно), Figma works at once. Checked at 1440/1024/768/390 by the overflow loop of `tyaga-block`.
- Prototype parity test skips the redrawn glyphs (`REDRAWN` in e2e_tyaga.py).

# Recipe: «Капля» (Kaplya Drop) — display font of merged ink drops. core_kaplya.js = kap/{engine,build,glyphs,api,tail}.js (mk_kaplya.sh), key sheet: keysheet.sh

Idea (refs r8: ability scale, Indigo weights, Botch): ONE design; fat, liquid strokes at every weight; each heavier weight spreads more (bulb ends, pooling, wobble, drips), spacing goes negative, Black = one fused stain. Light stays readable. Original letterforms, true lowercase, Latin + Cyrillic basic set.
1. Skeleton per glyph (kap/glyphs.js; outer box 0..W, centre-lines inset by o = stem/2; arcs kE, rounded polylines kRP, bowl/ring). Cyrillic reuses capital constructions; twins а е о р с у х are the Latin objects. Widths grow with stem (wdf); crowded glyphs (Ы ы И M « » s S quotes) get extra width so parts never merge.
2. Field (engine.js): round-cone capsules per stroke, polynomial smooth-min between strokes (k = 16+60·m^1.1, concave fillets), seeded 2-octave value noise (amp 2.5+0.034·capH·m^1.3, ×1.25 italic), marching squares at 8 u, 1-2-1 smooth, Schneider cubic fit split at extrema (tol 1.3; refit finer on self-intersection). 20–60 nodes/glyph.
3. Liquid strokes: free ends swell into balls (bm = 0.14+0.55·m^0.8, ≤ +62%, bottoms ×1.3, capped by the free gap to neighbours), 3–12% necking on long strokes, pooling toward the baseline. Horizontal strokes are thinner than verticals (field built in y-warped space, sy = 1 → 1.4 with stem) so counters survive heavy weights.
4. Weights (capH 700): stem Light 100 / Regular 134 / Bold 175 / Black 215 (0.14/0.19/0.25/0.31 capH; Bold = +31% Regular); melt 10/30/58/88; drip 8/28/55/80. Slider stem 80..min(250, 0.31·capH+0.2·(wdth-100)). Track = -0.27·stem·(m/0.3)^0.9 (Light -10, Regular -36, Black ≈ -150); sb 34, opsp 100, space 236+0.9|track|; no kerning table (kernMeasure's 12 u minimum gap would forbid the fusion).
5. Drops (`drip`): attached drips (neck r ≥ 15 + bead) from free ends / low bars, gate 0.14+0.6q per glyph, 1–2 max; Regular gate 0.25+0.6q with necks ≤ 0.12 xh, Light rare (0.12) tiny; a falling drop under the bead at drip ≥ 30%. Seeded by glyph id (stable).
6. Presets: Капля (default), Чистая (melt 0, drip 0), Пятно (melt 100, stem 215), Дождь (drip 100).
7. Safety net in kGlyph: reference = same glyph at stem 134/melt 30 (holes, parts). If the build has more holes or different parts, rebuild with 70/40/15/0% spread (and trimmed free ends) and keep the best.
## Misread rules (drops must not become diacritics or punctuation)
- Drip origin: a drip may start only where a stem/bowl bottom touches the baseline (or a descender end), never from a bar or open space; no detached satellite except a falling drop directly under its own drip bead (gap ≤ 0.11 xh); nothing detached between letters.
- No drip below the centre of C c С с S s Э э O o О о Q G g Є 0 @ & 8 (no cedilla / ogonek).
- stress_kaplya.js error `misread`: any satellite that is not entirely below the baseline directly under a body point (bead) within 0.14 xh.
## Experimental rules (relaxed on purpose, each with an allow-list)
- Topology changes with weight are intended: proof counts parts minus satellites (F.proof.drops, ui_c.js, computed upright slant 0); stemTol 0.2, hang, holeK 0.01.
- Counters may close only in F.allowClose (a e е ё з б в д р ф Ф ы ь э ю я Ж З 3 6 8 9 0 & № …) and only at melt ≥ 60 or stem ≥ 150; О Н П stay open. New counters (pockets) are never allowed.
- Narrow channels (closing gap ≤ 200 px at 4 u) are tolerated at stem ≥ 150 or melt ≥ 70; below that no slits/thin necks. Islands < 420 u² and holes with inscribed r < 16 u are removed.
- Negative sidebearings at Black are by design (track).
- Stress (stress_kaplya.js): parts == reference; holes == reference while melt ≤ 35 and stem < 150; drops ≥ 1500 u² and ≥ 14 u from body; no self-intersection; misread rule; thin/slit raster test. 150 combos × seeds 1,2,3 × regular+italic + presets = 0 bad.
Lessons: heavy stems break counters first (e s S б M № Ф): fix with y-warp, wider glyphs, aperture angles growing with stem, then the fallback rebuild; the reference must use the same xh as the design (xh grows with melt, which alone created a pocket in б). Detached dots read as diacritics/periods: keep them to the sides at heavy weights and carry the drop look with attached drips. Never pkill -f / pgrep -f with a pattern in the tool shell (matches itself).
- Digit 6/9 (K6 fix, 2026-10): 6 = low bowl (ry 0.27→0.205 cap with stem) + hook as quarter-ellipse arc 180→100° ending at the top at x = W·(0.74→0.66), glyph base width 600; the open upper-right notch makes 6 ≠ 0 at 12–24 px cover size (old 6: tall ellipse arc ended at top centre and fused with the bowl). 9 = rot(6). Check small sizes with qa_K6/r.sh style renders (cap 12–24 px) after any digit change.

# Recipe (DRAFT, round 1): «Кортес» (id `kortes`, working name) — heavy chiselled display caps with swash tails
Prototype, not integrated, caps only (lowercase code points map to caps). Files: kortes_core.js / kortes_letters.js / kortes_more.js / kortes_extra.js (generator, concatenated into kortes.js and inlined in proto.html), gen_json.js -> kortes.json, union3.py (painter-order polygon boolean + raster-trace fallback + Schneider curve refit), build_ttf.py -> kortes.ttf (+ kortes.fea), stress.py, misread.py, mk_alpha.py / mk_params.py / p_*.html (proofs). Genre (ParaType): декоративные / «готические» акцидентные антиква (heavy, faceted, wedge serifs). Rule zero kept: cap 700, stem, bars, serif atoms built first; unique layer = lens counters, wedge serifs, tails.

## Measured from ref_romarta.png (cap H = 164 px = 700 u, 1 px = 4.27 u)
- Stem (I) 32 px = 0.195 H = 137 u. Serif I ink 79 px = 0.48 H. Serif = flat-topped 44° wedge: 24 px tall (0.146 H = 102 u), reach 23.5 px (0.143 H = 100 u), straight faces, no bracket; sharp points at the tips.
- O ink 135 px = 0.82 H; side walls ≈ stem; top/bottom walls ≈ 0.06–0.08 H (hair ≈ 0.35–0.4 stem). Counter = a LENS leaning TL→BR (pointed tips top-left and bottom-right, left edge nearly straight). M 204 px = 1.24 H (incl. serifs), A 134 px = 0.82 H, L 121 px = 0.74 H, C 112 px = 0.68 H, S 119 px = 0.72 H.
- A: flat wide apex (~0.4 W) with a flag on the right, small triangular counter, bar top flat at ~0.48 H with a slanted underside. L: foot arm ends in an upward point. Spacing: serifs of neighbours almost touch (gap ≈ 0.03–0.05 H).
## Corrections to the first read
1. Not diagonal broad-pen stress: stems are vertical and thick, horizontals thin, the tilt lives only in the lens counters; outer shapes are near-symmetric.
2. Tails are signature on R (always long) — on C/G/O only a short foot at word end; so C G O Э tails are short and calt-gated to word end, R/Я long under the next letter.
3. Serifs are straight faceted wedges (one family: slope sh/sr, same wedge for feet, heads, beaks, bracket triangles, arm ends), not curved brackets.
4. Superior t/L are small raised letters; the dot is a diamond (all dots here: . , : ; ! ? Ё ·).

## Construction (kortes_*.js; UPM 1000, cap 700, desc -300 for tails/Д Щ, asc 960)
Glyph = painter-order list of polygons (add / subtract). Ring letters = rounded box minus slanted lens counter (O C G Q Э Ю Ф 0 6 8 9 @ ©); bowls (B D P R Б Ь Ъ Ы Я) = box with big right radii minus lens; straight letters = stems + wedge atoms (flB/flT/leg for sheared diagonals, bDR/bUR beaks, brD/brU bracket wedges); S 2 3 5 З 6 9 comma-family use an elliptical nib stroke (a = S/2, b = hair/2, 22–26°); tails = tapered blade polygons along a cubic. Cyrillic by structure, not borrowed: Д = Л + base bar with two wedge descenders, Ц Щ right descender beak, Й breve = crescent with thick ends (0.34 S) and thin middle, Б flag = long arm with beak, Ъ long left flag (Ъ/Ь/Б separated by the misread audit), У = long thin diagonal + thick arm (no vertical stem, ≠ Y, ≠ Ч), Ч = left half-stem + bar + full right stem (≠ 4), Ж = K + mirrored K on a central stem, З (round top, two bowls) vs 3 (flat top + diagonal) vs Э (mirrored C + tongue), Я = mirrored R (tail variant on the stem foot). Shared Cyrillic/Latin glyphs: А В Е К М Н О Р С Т Х І.

## OpenType (kortes.fea, verified in Chromium)
- calt: SWCTX — R, Я get .sw when followed by (letter|digit|swash-letter) +(letter|term), or by a term (space, NBSP, . ! ? : - – — ) » ” ’); C G O Э get .sw only before a term/space; nothing before Д Ц Щ Q , ; J (descender guard); tails never reach the baseline so no letter bodies are hit. NOTE: end-of-text without a trailing space/NBSP does not trigger (an `ignore sub` end-of-run rule did not work in HarfBuzz) — type a trailing NBSP or use ss01.
- ss01 / salt: .sw on every C G O R Э Я. ss02: all letters/digits -> .sup (superior, scale 0.5 raised to cap line, drawn with stem ×1.3). - kern (GPOS): 10 class pairs incl. .sw/.sup alternates (TA AT AV LT LY TO etc., Т Г Р before dot/comma). Only first-pass values; no automatic collision kerning yet.

## Round 3 (supersedes the swash rules of rounds 1–2)
- USER RULE: swash tails exist ONLY on R (Latin) and Я (Cyrillic); C G Э O have none; no word-end tails. SWASH = {R, Я}.
- Pair lock (calt `SWPAIR`, also `ss01`, no other forcing): R/Я -> `.sw` (long tail, ends ~0.75 of the next letter, under its bowl) before O C G Э; before Q -> `.swq` (tail 0.62x, so it stops before Q's own tail); before every other glyph, at word end, before space/punctuation: plain R/Я.

## Findings, ranked

### BLOCKER
1. **З ≡ 3 identical outlines in Volt, Kvadra, Устав, Модуль, Рывок** (previously known only for Volt/Kvadra). Also near-identical: Liniya .97, Rotunda .96 (Regular, 12 px). Expected: distinct (3 with flat top or З rounder/with closed top). Evidence: img/ev_Z3.png. Owner: core11/12/13 (Volt, Kvadra glyph table `З`/`3`), core_ustav_n1.js, core24.js, core_ryvok_n4.js (+ryvok_body.js), core18c.js/core25.js for the near-identical ones. Fix: make digit 3 a separate glyph (flat or angular top, open aperture) and keep З as the Cyrillic round form; add ≥10 % IoU gap test (<0.85) to check.sh.
2. **Рывок: words merge, horns collide, almost no kerning.** Word space 190 u (0.19 em) in a font whose letters are 1.2–1.8 em wide and whose horns overhang neighbours (H lsb -46); "СЛОВО — СЛО", "Й.С", "ЛК" read as one string; GPOS has only TA/LT class kerns, k=0 for Гд, «Т, Ta, Т., Р., «А (closest approach 400–580 u > word space). Evidence: img/kern_Regular.png (last two rows), img/ev_ustav_punct.png (bottom row). Owner: core_ryvok_n4.js (space, kern classes), ryvok_tail.js. Fix: space ≥ 330, add right-edge-aware class kerning for Г Т Р Ф Ж + lowercase/period/guillemets, clamp horn overhang so neighbours' horns do not touch (ink-overlap test in check).

### MAJOR
3. **Accents/breve/dot clearance does not scale with weight (Volt, Kvadra; partly Codex, Liniya, Propis).** Gap above caps/lc is a constant 30–42 u (Ё/Й 42, Latin 30–32) = 0.35 stem Light, 0.25 Regular, 0.18 Bold, 0.16 Black (rule ≥ 0.3×stem; Black needs ≥ 80 u). 111 composites measured; Volt Bold/Black ~35 glyphs below 0.3 stem; Liniya Å/å/Ů 20–22 u; Propis Black ã 8 u (<0.06 stem). Evidence: img/ev_accents_heavy.png; data m4.json. Owner: core17.js/core17x.js (shared diacritics, mark offset), core13.js (breve), core18a/b (Liniya/Propis marks). Fix: mark gap = max(0.3·stem, 40) derived from famParams stem.
4. **Kvadra (and Volt Black) horizontals stop growing: contrast rises with weight.** Kvadra O top 106/132/130/118 vs stem 118/168/232/266 → hz/stem .90/.79/.56/.44; Bold hz = Regular (0.98×); Black < Bold (0.91×). Volt hz 70/96/124/120 (Black < Bold 0.97×). Expected hz 80–90 % of stem in a neutral sans, monotonic. Evidence: img/ev_kvadra_weights.png. Owner: core_w.js famParams (hair/bar scaling) + core11/12 hair clamp (paramLimits core4 probably caps `hair`). Fix: hair ∝ stem for Kvadra (≥0.78), raise limit; Volt Black hair ≥ Bold.

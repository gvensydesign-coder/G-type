# Cyrillic + Latin letter proportions measured from open-licence fonts

## 0. Key findings (Regular, genre medians; details in sections 2-7)

- **x-height / capH**: oldstyle 0.69, didone 0.65, transitional 0.73, slab 0.73, neo-grotesque 0.75, humanist sans 0.75, geometric 0.71, condensed 0.74, mono 0.74, script 0.54. Ascender over capH: oldstyle +11%, transitional/slab +8-9%, sans 0-6%, didone/condensed 0%.
- **Stems**: H stem about 0.13 capH almost everywhere (geometric 0.11, mono 0.12, transitional and condensed 0.14); n stem 0.15-0.185 xH. Bold H stem 0.19-0.24 capH.
- **Contrast (thin/thick of O)**: didone 0.19, oldstyle 0.46, transitional 0.47, slab 0.82, humanist 0.82, condensed 0.85, mono 0.86, neo-grotesque 0.87, geometric 0.89. Real stress tilt only in oldstyle (about 7 deg lean-left); transitional/didone are vertical.
- **Widths vs Н (ink)**: serif genres are narrow, sans wide. Д 0.94-0.99 (serif) vs 1.21-1.27 (sans); Ж 1.37-1.46 vs 1.60-1.66; Ф 1.02-1.18 vs 1.26-1.39; Ш 1.37-1.42 vs 1.39-1.51; Ю 1.30-1.38 vs 1.49-1.57; Л 0.93-1.01 vs 1.07-1.12; О/Н 0.86-0.96 (serif/slab) vs 1.17-1.25 (sans).
- **Д**: every genre except script uses descending spur feet (depth 0.19-0.25 capH, serif deepest; scripts flush). Body: serif mostly trapezoid (top/base 0.80-0.84), sans mostly rectangle-like (0.85-0.88); triangular Д appears in geometric (Jost, Comfortaa, Tenor Sans), Forum and Ponomar.
- **Л**: all serif/slab/didone use a serif/bracketed left foot (kick 0.20-0.28 capH); sans (neo, humanist, condensed, mono) use a hook (kick 0.13-0.17); straight diagonal (Λ) Л in Comfortaa, Tenor Sans, Forum, Kelly Slab, Ponomar and all scripts (Ruslan Display is a heavy stub-leg decorative case).
- **Ц Щ tail**: 0.25 capH (serif), 0.22-0.23 (neo/humanist), 0.19-0.20 (condensed/geometric); the tail ends 0.11-0.15 capH to the right of the right stem (serif 0.14-0.15, sans 0.11-0.13).
- **б**: ascender equals the Latin l ascender (ratio about 1.0); flag tip stays near the top in serif/didone (0.94-0.96 of the ascender zone) and drops lower in sans (0.7-0.85); flag length 0.6-0.7 xH.
- **й breve**: thickness 0.08 xH (serif) vs 0.11 (sans); width 0.57 of body (serif) vs 0.68-0.75 (sans); clearance above xH 0.12-0.17 xH.
- **Spacing**: sans genres are loose, serif tight. н lsb 0.14-0.17 xH (sans) vs 0.08 (serif); mean gap in "нонинон" 0.24-0.28 xH (sans) vs 0.16-0.19 (serif); О lsb / Н lsb about 0.5-0.7 in sans, 0.96-1.22 in serif/slab/didone (serifs on Н push its sidebearing down to the О level).
- **Kerning**: all families except PT Mono carry GPOS kern; pair counts range from a few thousand (decorative) to about 150k (oldstyle).


Measured 60 families / 105 styles (Regular = wght 400, Bold = wght 700 or static Bold) with fontTools only (outlines flattened, own nonzero-winding scanline slicer, 1000-UPM units). Fonts come from github.com/google/fonts (sparse checkout); variable fonts were instantiated at wght 400/700 with other axes at default (opsz default, wdth 100). Nothing from the downloads was executed.

Files: `db.json` (all raw measures per family/style, incl. licence, source URL, genre, per-glyph advance/ink/bearings for all of А-Я а-я Ёё A-Z a-z), `genre_summary.csv` (long: genre, style, measure, n, median, q1, q3, min, max for ~870 measures), `genre_median_iqr_wide.csv`, `measures_regular_flat.csv` (one row per family), `category_counts.json`, `sheets/contact_<genre>.png` (Д Л Ж Ф б д ц й for every family, annotated with the classification), `code/` (measurement code).

Conventions: all lengths are ratios of capH (capitals) or xH (lowercase) unless stated; widths are INK widths relative to the ink width of Cyrillic Н (caps) or н (lowercase); "median (Q1-Q3)". Genre sample sizes are small (3-8), so quartiles are indicative; the ALL column pools all families.

## 8. Method, validation and caveats

- Outlines are flattened (14 segments per curve) and cut with horizontal/vertical scanlines using nonzero winding; counters (enclosed empty regions) are found by flood fill on a 160-row raster, which also handles overlapping contours in variable-font instances. Lengths are scaled to 1000 UPM. capH = ink top of H, xH = ink top of x, ascender = max top of b d h l, descender = max depth of p q.
- Stems: median of first-run width of H (y 0.18-0.38 capH) and n (0.2-0.42 xH); bars: vertical slice through the glyph centre; O thick = max side stroke around mid-height, O thin = thinnest top/bottom stroke over x = 25-75%; axis from the mean x of near-thinnest points at top vs bottom.
- Д: foot style from run count 0.5 depth below baseline (or 0.03 capH when depth < 0.04 capH); extension = foot outer edge vs body at 0.2 capH; shape from top width at 0.8 capH / base width at 0.2 capH. Л: leg tracked downwards from 0.4 capH; slant from right-edge fit; kick = how far the leftmost ink sits left of the leg line below 0.2 capH; type diagonal if |slant| > 0.18, otherwise hook, and serif when the font has serifs (H foot width > 1.5 stems). Ж К: junction = y band where arms touch the stem (one run or minimum gap), arms from run centres above/below the band. Ц Щ: tail from the slice at 60% of the depth, offset against the right stem at mid-height. б: flag tip = right-most ink above xH. Я ы: bowl outer from vertical slices through the left-most counter. й: breve = contours entirely above 1.02 xH (not available when merged into the base, e.g. Lobster, Ruslan Display).
- Visual validation: four families (PT Sans, EB Garamond, Roboto Slab, Playfair Display) were checked by eye against overlays of the measured heights (`val2.png`) before the batch; further fixes made after eye-checks: Л logic (replaced hook/serif width test, which misclassified Montserrat/Noto Sans), Ж junction (arms that touch the stem at a minimum gap, Spectral), counter detection for overlapping contours (Alegreya Ф), g storey for open-loop g (Playfair, Prata), Э/З terminals (both open to the left). All eleven contact sheets were looked at.
- Caveats: genre samples are small (3-8), so use medians as defaults and the IQR as plausible range; Ж/К arm angles for serif fonts include terminals (serif arms curve, so their angle is larger than the stem direction); axis angle is noisy for contrast > 0.7 and only reported below 0.8; Merriweather, Source Serif 4, Literata, Alegreya, Spectral etc. use default optical size; Roboto, Open Sans, Noto, IBM Plex Sans, Tektur use default width.
- Kerning samples come from the GPOS `kern` feature of the instantiated font (first matching PairPos subtable); contextual / script-specific kerning is not evaluated.

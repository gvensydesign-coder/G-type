# Type design foundation

Rule zero: never invent a typeface from scratch. Build the standard construction first (metrics, control glyphs, components, spacing, kerning, OpenType), verify it, and only then apply the unique design layer (style parameters, signature forms). Unique decisions are deltas on a sound base. Use ParaType terminology (section 16) in every UI label, tag and report to the user; the product word for the whole thing is «шрифт» («Скачать шрифт»), «гарнитура» stays in ParaType definitions only. Exception: experimental (non-classical) fonts may break classic rules on purpose, within reason and only through an explicit allow-list (section 18a).

Map: sections 1–10 rules; 11–11c-3 recipes, generator vocabulary and measured defaults; 12–15 workflows and tools; 16–16h digests of the knowledge bases; 17–18 references and project use; 18a experimental allow-list; 18b per-typeface owner decisions; 19 where this lives in G-type (KB index `kb/`, repo documents, build, service facts, verification lines).

## 1. Workflow (fixed order)
1. **Brief**: genre by ParaType classification (section 16), use (text/display), scripts (Latin, Cyrillic, extended), charset (basic or extended) and target languages, weights/styles, user rules. Fix the гарнитурные признаки up front (ParaType, `kb/PARATYPE_KB.md`): proportions, contrast, aperture, oval-axis angle, caps : lowercase ratio, width variation (разноширинность), serif and terminal forms — they are the acceptance criteria for the genre check (QA 11).
2. **Metrics**: UPM 1000; cap height, x-height, ascender, descender, overshoot; vertical metrics policy (section 3). Start from the measured genre defaults (section 11c).
3. **Construction system**: stem, hairline/horizontal, contrast axis, round vs straight ratio, terminals, joins, apertures. Store as parameters, not per-glyph numbers.
4. **Control glyphs**: H O n o (+ Н О н о, then a e s g). Approve these before anything else.
5. **Components**: stems, bowls/rings, arches, diagonals, terminals, serifs, dots, diacritic marks. Every glyph is assembled from components; shared Latin/Cyrillic forms are one glyph.
6. **Full set by families**: n-family (n h m u r), o-family (o c e d b p q), diagonals (v w x y k z), caps by structure (straight/round/diagonal), figures, punctuation, symbols, Cyrillic specifics, diacritics.
7. **Spacing** (section 5), then **kerning** (section 6).
8. **OpenType + export** (sections 8–9).
9. **QA** (section 10). Only now: **unique design layer** → re-run QA.

## 2. Anatomy and optics (rules)
- Overshoot (свисание): round and pointed forms exceed the flat lines by ~1–3% of the height (10–15 u at UPM 1000 for x-height 500). Apex/vertex of A V W M also overshoot or are cut.
- Horizontals look heavier than verticals of the same thickness: horizontals 80–90% of stem in a sans; more contrast in serif.
- Round stems are heavier at the extremes than straight stems (O side ≈ 105–110% of H stem); thin the bowl where it joins a stem (ink-trap logic, b d p q n). Ink traps (ловушки) where inner angles are < 30°.
- Optical center: crossbars of E F H sit slightly above geometric middle; upper bowls (B, 8, 3, S) smaller than lower; the optical centre of O sits above its geometric centre, and the oval axis runs through the thin places (`kb/TYPO_KARPENKO_KB.md`). Pure geometry without optical corrections (Futura-style) makes letters «walk» in proportion — always correct optically.
- Diagonals: optically as strong as the stems. Measure thickness perpendicular to the stroke axis and compensate: Л У Х К must not look thinner than the verticals, И Й Ж must not look heavier (owner remarks on Столб and И/Й). Thin only inside the joint itself (A V W X K), with pen-ellipse width, not a constant offset.
- Round caps are slightly wider than straight caps' optical width; O is the widest base cap after M W.
- Lowercase vs caps: same construction logic, caps stems ~5–10% heavier than lowercase stems in text faces.
- Joins in heavy weights: notch or taper where arches enter stems so counters stay open (join compensation).
- Apertures (c e s a): open more in heavy and condensed styles. Measure the aperture as the gap between the terminals of С S e plus the cut angle of those terminals (closed / semi-closed / open), not as counter area; terminals of a closed-aperture face keep one small constant gap.
- Axis (ось овалов): ~30° from vertical in broad-nib faces (measured on real old-style text fonts the O tilt is smaller, ≈ 7°), vertical in modern/neo-grotesque; stress (наплыв) sits perpendicular to it. Measure the main axis on O. Keep the axis constant across weights: with a nib engine a constant nib angle drifts (27→44° Light→Black), so compensate the nib angle per weight. Uncials may use a near-flat pen (axis ≈ 0°, horizontal contrast) — state it as a design property.
- Serifs (засечки): нескруглённая (abrupt) / скруглённая (adnate, bracket = скругление); shapes hairline · curve · slab · offset slab · compound (flaring); вертикальная засечка (beak) on С Е Г Т and Latin a c j y F E S (ParaType). Serif form must follow the tool/construction of the whole face.
  - One serif family per face: every serif, spur, tick, tail end, flag end and beak is built from the face's own wedge helpers (same angle, taper, bracket). A rectangular or round-ended spur added later reads as foreign at once — users notice it.
  - Serifs on diagonal stems are sheared along the stem (no stepped edges); serif faces meeting a thin stroke are trimmed along the thin stroke's axis, built as one polygon, not a flat pen cap.
  - A stroke plus a separately drawn polygon (flag, ribbon) creates kinks at the join — fold the stem into the polygon, matched to the neighbouring bowl width.
  - Flags/ribbons (б, r, ф head) are polygons with a swelling body and a tapered, slightly upturned tip; widening pen nodes next to a stem fattens the stem instead.
  - Long curved single strokes (&, S, g tails): evenly spaced nodes with tangents matching the chord; few nodes with mismatched tangents bow the stroke, uneven spacing wobbles.
- Broad-nib engines: an elliptical pen makes BOTH diagonals thick — the thin diagonal (/ in a right-handed nib) needs its own thin pen; contrast is set by hair/stem ratio (measured old-style O thin/thick ≈ 0.46; 1:9 vanishes at 14–18 px).
- Italic: slant 8–12° with optical correction (rotate rounds slightly, keep stems' weight); true italic (курсив) forms where the genre requires; a slanted roman is «наклонное» (oblique), not italic; «косой» is a back-slant (cartography), never a default. Classic italics: capitals slant slightly less than lowercase; most italics 2°–20°.
- Tangents: every curve-to-curve and curve-to-line join is G1 (collinear handles) unless it is an intended corner. Nodes on extremes (H/V tangents). No kinks, steps, or micro-segments.
- Bezier quality: handle length ~0.55 of the segment for circle-like arcs (κ = 0.5523 for a circle); "superellipse" tension 0.6–0.75 for squarish grotesks; avoid handles crossing.

## 3. Metrics and vertical policy
- Typical (UPM 1000): cap 700, x 500–540, asc 720–760, desc −200..−250. Digital fonts are designed at a base size of ~28 pt. Genre-specific x/cap, ascender and descender: section 11c.
- Small x-height (очко) goes with long extenders; at one size a large-x-height face looks bigger and leaves less air between lines — set leading and size comparisons by x-height, not by cap height (`kb/TYPO_KARPENKO_KB.md`).
- hhea ascender/descender = OS/2 typo = real ink bbox of the set (or typo with USE_TYPO_METRICS bit 7); winAscent/winDescent ≥ max/min ink so nothing clips.
- lineGap 0; total ascender+|descender| ≈ 1.2× UPM for comfortable default leading.
- Accents on caps may exceed winAscent only if winAscent covers them. Editors must show the accent zone (up to cap + 0.35 em).
- Cap height, x-height, ascender and descender are CONSTANT across weights of one family (heavy weights must not grow taller); flat and round tops/bottoms are warped back to the zone lines, marks are clamped to a fixed mark zone. hhea/typo values must not change with weight.

## 4. Cyrillic and Latin together
- Shared forms are identical glyphs/components: А В Е К М Н О Р С Т Х, а е о р с у х, і ј ѕ.
- A shared glyph must read correctly in BOTH scripts: a Latin-genre form that reads as another Cyrillic letter is a bug (round uncial E reads as Є). Check every shared glyph inside Cyrillic words.
- Cyrillic specifics (follow Zhukov/ParaType practice):
  - Д Л Ц Щ: descenders on the right (Ц Щ) or both feet (Д); Л with a hooked or straight left leg matching the style.
  - Ж К: arms match K; Ж symmetric, knot (узел) at optical center, not overloaded in heavy weights.
  - Б: top flag the length of Г's arm; б lowercase has an ascending flag/curve, never a scaled Б.
  - И Й: the diagonal is drawn a little thinner than the stems so that it looks optically equal to them; a full-width diagonal reads heavy (owner remark). **Cyrillic breve on Й/Ў has thickened ENDS** (teardrop ends in serif faces) — unlike the Latin breve, which thickens in the middle. Small, accent weight, never a spike.
  - Ы Ь Ъ Ю Я: bowls from Р/Ь component; Я is mirrored R logic, not a literal mirror. Ы = Ь + I with a normal inner gap; Ъ must differ clearly from Ь (flag).
  - З opens to the left and must differ from Э and from 3 (give З a waist/middle notch and a different top terminal than 3, or give 3 a flat top); У must differ from Ч and 4 (a curled left arm reads as Ч — use a straight diagonal).
  - Latin l: a short tail or none — a long foot reads as L; I/l/1 must stay distinguishable at 12 px.
  - Lowercase в д к л м н п т in roman are small caps–like but with lowercase stem weight and x-height optics; italic forms (д g-like, т m-like, и u-like, п n-like) for true italics/scripts. In roman, lowercase д is the printed form (flat roof with serifs, stem, two feet; or the ∂/ковш form): a g-like descending д reads as Latin g (дом → gom) and a capital Д scaled down is the wrong letter (`kb/AFISHA_LESSONS_v2.md`; owner remark of 2 Nov 2026).
  - Measured Cyrillic structure per genre (Д feet and body, Л leg, Ц Щ tails, б flag, й breve, Ё dots, З Э waists and terminals, Ж К junctions, Я ы ь ъ bowls): section 11c-2; the professional serif constructions of б д ж: section 16h.
  - У/у: Cyrillic у ≈ Latin y; capital У ≠ Y (straight left arm) — except in unicase display faces where a shared form is part of the design.
  - Ё is mandatory in dictionaries, education, ambiguous words; the diaeresis must clear the cap.
- Latin specifics: n and r are arch/shoulder forms; m is triple arch. Project rule (owner, 15 Nov 2026): Latin m is always the original arched Latin form drawn from the font's own n (stem, arch, joint, serifs), never a copy of Cyrillic м; a Cyrillic-style m may exist only as ss01. Latin n and r are likewise original Latin forms, never copies of п and г. W needs diagonals (two V's), not Ш logic; G needs a spur/bar so it doesn't read as C.
- When a reference shows only one script (or another script entirely, e.g. Japanese), design Cyrillic and Latin in the same construction logic, never by borrowing a generic form.

## 5. Spacing
- Terms: апрош = space between letters; полуапрош = sidebearing (left/right of each glyph); толщина литеры = advance; ширина знака = ink width; трекинг = uniform change; разрядка = letterspacing for emphasis.
- Method (Tracy, "Letters of Credit"): set n and o first: n side bearings from stem (left ≈ right, slightly less on arch side); o both sides ≈ 70–80% of n's straight side. H and O likewise. Straight sides = n/H, round sides = o/O, diagonal/open sides ≈ minimal.
- Area method (HT Letterspacer): equalize white area between glyph and a reference depth zone; good for automating, then correct optically.
- Test strings: nnonnoo / HHOHHOO / nnxnn / Ннонно / words with heavy rhythm (minimum, шиншилла, Иллюминация).
- Word space ≈ 1/3 em (Latin ≈ width of i); spaces needed in every font: space, NBSP (U+00A0), thin space U+2009 (≈ 1/5–1/8 em), NNBSP U+202F.
- Display faces: tighter; text faces: looser. Heavy weights need slightly more sidebearing. Wide letters with tails (Ж Ш Щ Ц Д) need their own width/sidebearing check. Measured: sans are spaced looser than serif faces (section 11c, calibration numbers in 11c-3).
- Word space: ≈ 1/3 em is the target; the m29 audit measured 190–250 u in all nine fonts and asked 270–330 u for text-capable faces (`kb/AUDIT_m29.md`). Display faces may sit at 190–240 u only while words still separate (kerning check 23: no pair gap above 0.8 × word space); Рывок needs ≥ 330 because its horns hang into the neighbour space.
- j, f tails may extend into neighbors (боковое свисание); kern collisions after descenders/ascenders.
- Ornaments that project sideways: space from the letter BODY, let the projection hang into neighbour space, kern by body-band gap with a full-profile collision guard.
- Uniwidth (униширинные) option: identical advances across weights/italic for UI fonts; monospaced = one advance for all glyphs.

## 6. Kerning
- Kern only after spacing is right. Use classes by shape: straight, round, diagonal, open-top (T F P), open-bottom (L), etc.
- Priority pairs: caps (AV AT LT LY PA TA VA WA YA), cap+lc (Ta Te To Va Ye), punctuation (T. V, F. P. „ » « “), figures (11, 17, 74), Cyrillic (ГА ТА УА ЛТ Г. Т. Ту Гд), quotes.
- Exceptions override classes; a class value = median of its pairs, outliers become exceptions. Keep values ≤ ~15% UPM; never let kerning create collisions.
- Localized/alternate/small-cap glyphs join the kerning classes of their base glyphs (small caps: scaled cap classes).
- For connecting scripts never kern across a connection.
- Shared kerning floors/caps tuned for one genre saturate another: if many different pairs (AV TA LT To Ya) get the identical value, the gap floor or depth cap is clamping — give the font its own gap/depth/strength hooks and re-verify pairs visually.
- Digits: per-digit sidebearing additions only on proportional figures; tabular figures keep one advance; do not kern tabular digits.
- Calibration targets (per 1000 capH, medians of 60 open families, negative = tighter): АТ −85, ТА −83, AV −87, LT −101, ГА −100, УА −97, To −95, Yo −100, Г. −123, Т. −102, Ау −33, ТО −17; slab faces kern hardest (AV −188, Г. −187), neo-grotesque and humanist sans lightest on AV (−96 / −56). Full table and per-genre ranges: section 11c-3. A class value far outside its genre range needs a reason.
- After kerning, the closest approach of an open-right letter (Р Г Ф 7 Ж Т) to a period/comma must stay below 0.8 × word space; the m29 audit found 270–470 u gaps after Р. Г. Ф. 7. and fixed them with a class of −150…−200 against open-right letters (raise the kern cap per font, not globally).
- Autokern is computed lazily and cached: opening the editor never waits for it (12 Nov 2026 lesson, the editor opened slowly because of autokern). Under «Искажение» it is computed on the undistorted glyphs (section 19).

## 7. Diacritics and composites
- Build from anchors (якоря): base + mark at top/bottom anchor (bbox centre or optical centre, clearance ≈ 30 u above, ogonek/cedilla overlap the base); caps use cap-height mark variants (flatter, lower); i/j use dotless bases.
- Marks share stroke weight with the face; dots round/square per the face; mark height ≈ 0.15–0.25 cap height, width ≤ 0.8 of the base.
- Ogonek, cedilla, comma-below attach to the base outline; caron on ď ť ľ Ľ is the apostrophe form with clearance.
- Check heavy and light weights: no fusion with base — gap ≥ max(40 u, 0.32×stem) + overshoot — no detached slivers. Measure the gap on the real outline of the letter (Ё Й in every weight), not on the height lines (12 Nov 2026 skill update); the m29 audit measured Volt/Kvadra gaps of 0.35 / 0.25 / 0.18 / 0.16 stem for Light / Regular / Bold / Black.
- Marks scale with the pen: dots ≈ 0.6 × stem, lifted ≥ 0.3 × stem above a horn or ring top; at Black the gap is ≥ 80 u. Cyrillic breve: thick ends (≥ 0.4 × stem), middle ≥ 0.35 × stem — a 14–18 u hairline middle (Устав, stem 110) turns Й into И below 60 px. Marks shrink with weight instead of rising (FIX_INTEGRATE: Rotunda Ё tops Light…Black 805/859/920/961 → 809/852/894/893; not fully constant, Пропись й Black still 22 u over its zone).
- Coverage baseline (extended charset): Latin-1, Latin Extended-A, Cyrillic basic + Ukrainian, Belarusian, Serbian/Macedonian (Ђ Ј Љ Њ Ћ Џ Ѕ, locl forms), Kazakh/Bashkir/Tatar if requested. Report support per LANGUAGE (CLDR main exemplars) with the exact missing letters.
- Basic charset (display faces on request): A–Z a–z, А–Я а–я Ёё, 0–9, . , : ; ! ? « » „ “ ” ‘ ’ " ' - – — ‒ … − ( ) / & №, space, NBSP, thin space, NNBSP. Declare it as a font property.
- Punctuation in the face's logic: « » real chevrons, „“ from the comma (hanging below the baseline), “ ‘ ” ’ tops aligned, … one glyph (tighter than three periods), − on figure width and the + bar axis, ‒ (figure dash) at digit width, & distinct from 8, ? with a clean hook, № with an open o, 1 carrying stem weight.
- Punctuation scale (m29 audit, all measured on real fonts): hyphen < en < em, and an em dash is wider than any single letter (Рывок: minus 655 > em 582 was a defect); hyphen height ≈ x-height / 2; minus on the + bar axis (Liniya/Propis were +28 u high); „ hangs around the baseline (Volt −126…170), “ ‘ ” ’ share one top at cap height (“ at 730 over a 700 cap was a defect); comma/semicolon tail ≤ −150 u and quotes ≤ ~250 u tall (Устав comma at −333…−457 pushed winDescent to 486 and the line to 1.52 em); « » not larger than the letter (Rotunda 640 × 467 was flagged); U+2012 = advance of 0 with the bar of the minus.
- Extended punctuation in the face's logic: § with half-em space, ¶ † ‡, asterism, ¤ for a currency without its own sign, bullet at the middle of the x-height (a large bullet at cap-height middle), hairline rule = 1/4 pt (`kb/PARATYPE_KB.md`, `kb/TYPO_KARPENKO_KB.md`).

## 8. OpenType features
- kern (GPOS, classes + pairs), mark/mkmk if marks are separate; liga (fi fl ff ffi ffl) only in Latin text faces — Russian uses no ligatures (except deliberate display constructor ligatures, section 11); case; tnum/pnum, lnum/onum (all 4 combinations; currency signs on tabular width); smcp/c2sc; frac (numr/dnom + fraction bar U+2044 at ~45°, not the slash), sups/subs/sinf, ordn; salt/swsh for scripts; ss01–ss20 named; calt for connecting scripts; locl for Serbian/Bulgarian.
- Small caps (капитель): true small caps, not scaled capitals — height ≈ x-height + 3–8%, slightly wider than caps, stem weight = lowercase stems, built from the same components. Useful in Cyrillic (а б е р с у ф differ); used with oldstyle figures.
- Oldstyle figures (минускульные): 6 8 ascend, 3 4 5 7 9 descend; lining (маюскульные) at cap height. Oldstyle in running text and with small caps, lining in tables and reports (`kb/TYPO_KARPENKO_KB.md`).
- Ligatures: Latin fi fl ff ffi ffl æ œ in text faces; discretionary ct st only in italics and scripts. Display constructor ligatures (Сцепка) are the one allowed Russian case: each in an explicit pair list, an off-switch stylistic set (ss01) that sits first in the lookup list, a dense set (ss02), and the preview shaper must emulate GSUB in the same order as HarfBuzz.
- Letter variants (service feature «Варианты букв»): 2–4 alternates per letter chosen by neighbours through `calt` and named sets ss01–ss03 (same mechanism as Сцепка and Кортес); identical letters in a row never get the same variant; alternates join the kerning classes of their base; check the exported file in a shaper, not only the preview. «Искажение» variants (up to 5, off by default) are screen-only and are not written to the file.
- Ship pro features (smcp/c2sc, onum/lnum, tnum/pnum, frac, sups/subs, salt/swsh) only for extended-charset fonts; a basic display set gets kern + locl only. New alternates join kerning classes and are filtered identically in static and variable builders.
- Substitutions must be reversible and not break shaping across scripts; browsers split script runs, so calt cannot connect Cyrillic to Latin in one word. Glyph names follow AGL (a.sc, one.osf, one.tf, one.numr, uni2044).

## 9. Naming, passport and export
- Name IDs 1/2 (RIBBI: Regular/Italic/Bold/Bold Italic as one family), 4, 6 (ASCII PostScript; transliterate Cyrillic), 16/17 for other weights, ID 10 description; usWeightClass 100–900; usWidthClass from proportions; fsSelection bits; italicAngle negative for right slant; post format 2. File names and unique ID follow the user's font name.
- OS/2 passport: ulUnicodeRange/ulCodePageRange from the cmap (CP1251, CP1252, CP1250…), sFamilyClass, 10-digit PANOSE computed from parameters (family kind, serif, weight, proportion, contrast, stroke variation, arm, letterform, midline, x-height), fsType 0, x/cap height; gasp (smoothing small, grid-fit+smooth from ~9 ppem). Prove metadata-only changes by comparing glyf/hmtx/GSUB/GPOS byte-identity.
- Hinting: do not ship hand-rolled TrueType instructions you cannot verify at 9–14 px; prefer ttfautohint offline, otherwise state «no hinting» honestly.
- Weights (насыщенность): сверхсветлое Thin, светлое Light 300, нормальное Regular 400, полужирное Medium/SemiBold, жирное Bold 700, сверхжирное Black 900. **Bold stem ≥ 1.25× Regular** (ParaType rule for emphasis); Light < Regular < Bold < Black strictly. Stem/x-height ≈ 0.22 / 0.32 / 0.44 / 0.53 for sans; compensate apertures, counters, joins and width as weight grows. Display faces may be heavy-only.
  - Check the Bold/Regular ratio on the stem, on the o ring and on the horizontals: ring ratios ran lower than stem ratios (Устав stem 1.29 / o 1.14, Модуль 1.38 / 1.15 in the m29 audit). Light and Regular must differ visibly (Рывок Light ≈ Regular, 1.12, was flagged); per-font stem tables fix it. Live Рывок ladder (core_ryvok_n4.js): stem = 0.18 / 0.245 / 0.33 / 0.38 × capH.
  - Documented exceptions under 1.25 are per-font allow-list entries, not a relaxed rule: Сцепка Bold/Regular 1.10 (stem stops at 164 where counters of Ш Щ Ж close), Тяга ≈ 1.13 (computed from its ladder 18.75 → 21.1 → 23). Everything else is ≥ 1.25.
- Proportions (by Н width/height): сверхузкое · узкое (40–60%) · нормальное · широкое (≥ 100%) · сверхширокое.
- Variable fonts: masters point-compatible; axes wght, wdth, opsz (оптический размер), slnt/ital; overlapping contours allowed (OVERLAP_SIMPLE); intermediate masters where an outline would self-cross; filter alternates identically in static and variable builders; suffix VF/Variable.
  - N-axis model (fontTools VariationModel): masters at axis extremes plus corner masters (Light/Regular/Bold/Black × Narrow/Wide) so every named instance is an exact master; fvar + avar + STAT, Russian (0x419) and English instance names.
  - opsz 8–72 (default 14) mapped logarithmically through avar; small sizes = wider sidebearings, more space, heavier hairlines, larger x-height, open apertures, positive tracking.
  - wdth range per font: probe the parameter limits — raise the axis minimum until Bold/Black stems keep ≥ 88% of the requested value, otherwise the weight axis collapses at the narrow edge.
  - slnt (−12…0) is an oblique shear: offer it only when the italic uses the same construction; a true cursive italic (different forms) stays a separate static/ital file. Drop slnt for a font whose sheared instances drift from the static italic beyond tolerance.
  - Per-glyph defect fallback (drop opsz → slnt → wdth → static) on contour flips, contact between contours or XOR vs static > limit; verify named instances and ~20 random points against static builds; kerning in a VF stays static unless GPOS is varied.
  - Parameter ladders keyed by named weights (e.g. a per-weight hairline table) must also be applied to VF masters at arbitrary weights (interpolate between neighbouring named weights by weight class), or static and VF outputs diverge.
  - Uniwidth (униширинные): every weight/italic keeps Regular's advances; re-space by the lsb/rsb ratio, rebuild overflowing glyphs narrower first, x-compress last (report % per glyph), add positive kerns where pairs collide.
- post.italicAngle is negative for right slant and non-zero ONLY in italic files (an upright file with a non-zero angle breaks fsSelection/macStyle consistency); a script whose skeleton is already slanted reports the visible slant via a per-font hook.
- Decorative styles (декоративные начертания) derived from outlines: контурное (outline), оконтуренное (inline / outer contour), оттенённое (shaded/3D, solid or hatched), выворотное (cameo). Offset by flatten → offset (miter/round) → exact boolean → refit; clamp offsets by the font's min stem/counter; node count ≤ ~4.5× (7× hatch); separate family names («<Family> Outline»). Every new font runs the deco lane in all weights/italics: union the result and drop sliver loops (small area or mean width) so no style ships broken.
- Validate with fontTools (load, save, compare, instancer vs static), render-test in a browser with features on/off.

## 10. QA checklist (run before any delivery)
1. Topology: expected contours/holes; no detached parts, stray micro-contours, wrong direction.
2. Outline quality: G1 joins, extrema nodes (экстремальные точки), no steps/kinks/notches, inner angles < 20° blunted by a 1–3 u segment, exact verticals/horizontals, no double-inflection or looped curves, node count reasonable (sans ≤ ~20/glyph, script ≤ ~30, spiked display ≤ ~40, liquid display ≤ ~60; faceted wedge display up to ~75 — Кортес average 18, maximum 74; hand-line fonts built with an exact union and simplify tolerance 0.3, Нить, carry no node cap). Pro serif rings are 4 + 4 nodes, n 22–26, e 12–17, a 20–31, r/y 18–29 (section 16h).
3. Zones: nothing pokes outside its zone except intended overshoots and extenders.
4. Consistency: stems/horizontals within ±3% of system; shared Cyrillic/Latin glyphs identical; caps/lowercase share construction.
5. Counters: none closed in any weight; apertures readable at 12 px.
6. Spacing/kerning: test strings even; no collisions in 40+ critical pairs; no pair gap that reads as a word space.
7. Diacritics: clearance in all weights, anchors, caps variants, Cyrillic breve form.
8. Export: fontTools round-trip, names/weights/metrics/passport correct, features work in Chromium.
9. Parameter robustness: random/extreme sweeps keep topology identical to defaults; interdependent limits; fix with adaptive geometry first; every slider must actually change the output.
10. Visual: proof sheets per weight/style, text RU/EN at 16/48/120 px; the lead's eye on key images incl. the real editor view.
11. Genre check: reference crop vs ours side by side; compare key ratios with the genre medians (section 11c); reject «clean but generic».
12. Isolation: a font's sliders must not leak to other fonts or change their random-test sequences.
13. Digits and small glyphs: render at 12–24 px cap height (library covers, UI chips) — a closing counter turns 6/9 into 0, 3 into 8.
14. Misread audit (различимость): next to a plain reference font, list every glyph readable as another letter (Є/Е, Э/З/3, Ч/У/4, П/А, Λ/Л, Л/7, Ш/W, C/G, 8/&, П/М, м/т, I/l/1, l/L, O/0, ив/иб, р/п, ь/ъ). 16. Typography completeness: Russian quotes «»„“, — – ‒ - − distinct, …, №, NBSP/thin space, Ё, breve form, Bold ≥ +25%, constant vertical metrics across weights.
17. Typographic colour (цветность): a justified paragraph has even grey value; no rivers.

Raw cap-wide spreads are structural (T Г 7 light, B В 8 heavy); fix peers first, then balance outliers via width/hair, never distort proportions. Report the remaining spread honestly.

20. Verify that an edit actually renders: generic builders (punctuation, accents, derived glyphs) may redefine a glyph after your definition — compare before/after renders, not just code.
21. After every round re-render ALL approval sheets and look at each one; a sheet that was regenerated but not viewed is not checked.
22. Variable fonts: every font with a «Вариативный» tag is in the VF test lane — gvar non-empty for every glyph, masters point-compatible, named instances vs statics XOR ≤ 2–3 % at 200 px, hhea/OS/2 vertical metrics equal to the static family. A dead axis = drop the VF and its tag.

Editor tooling: preset values clamped to each font's effective limits, active state from clamped values, duplicate-output chips disabled; slider tracks equal the effective range; defaults inside the range; the kerning value shown in the UI must equal the exported GPOS value (automated round-trip test); undo works with a focused control.

Then verify each branch's key fixes on renders.

## 11. Construction recipes (parametric)
- **Grotesk**: rectangle stems, superellipse rings (tension 0.6–0.75), flat terminals cut horizontally or at a fixed angle, joins notched in heavy weights, diagonals by pen-ellipse width.
- **Geometric/monoline**: skeleton paths + round pen (offset curve with adaptive subdivision and Bezier refit), circles κ=0.5523, round or flat caps, joins round. Ligatures in monoline faces fuse crossbars at low hair — bridge or drop them.
- **Broad-nib (uncial, humanist serif)**: skeleton + elliptical/flat nib at fixed angle (20–35°); serifs as pen entry/exit, wedges; contrast from nib, not drawn thicks.
- **Pointed-pen script**: skeleton + pressure by direction (downstrokes thick, upstrokes hairline), slant 15–22°, connections at one height, entry/exit strokes, loops on descenders; swashes (росчерки) via swsh/salt with smooth junctions. Do not lift cap/x/descender heights with weight.
- **Rotunda (round gothic, Polanco-type)**: colour first — stem/xh ≈ 0.34, hair ≈ 0.06 xh, nib 28–38° (compensated per weight to hold the axis), compact width, short ascenders, tight spacing (sb ≈ 42). Counters become lancets from the nib. Stem ends cut along the nib; never cut a stroke with a near-parallel line. Diamond dots. Е must be stem + bars (uncial E reads as Є); M straight stems + wedge; 1 with a low flat flag. Rejected on a pen-width engine: two-arc lancet bowls, rotunda feet (need dedicated terminal code).
- **Condensed Old-Russian display (ustav-inspired)**: skeleton + elliptical pen, fat verticals, hairline horizontals, flare via node weights, lancet bowls, thin sloped crossbars (rise ≤ 0.1 h), drop serifs ≤ 0.26 h, square dots, unicase; width ~95%; У straight diagonal arm; Е short tick (≤ 0.05 h). Exempt sloped bars (incl. H/Н) from bar checks. Classify as «Древнерусские · Устав», never «Вязь». Details: Ъ/ъ need width 440 at wdth 92; narrower = DEFAULTS wdth 95 (italic 92) and limit 92, not the width factor (0.95 closed B P Ы Ь 8 r); Latin l is tailed (I/l IoU 1.00 → 0.86); hygiene `HYG.ustav` flat 8 and minSeg 0 (a real chisel flat stays above the 6 u lint threshold); live VF axes wght, wdth, opsz.
- **Modular grid display**: heavy slab minus keyholes; one stroke module; cell classes; unicase; tight modular spacing; overlap glued parts by 1–1.5 u. Diagonal letters from bands/wedge pockets; band cap ~0.3 w for Black. Traps: A needs a bar; З/3 a right waist notch; Y/У a band fork; W two V bands; G bar shelf + spur; M V to 0.5 h.
- **Spiked monumental display («Рывок»)**: heavy wide unicase grotesk, slit counters; horns grow out of the stroke (full stroke-end base, one straight + one concave edge, tip 28–34 u), along baseline/bars, placed per glyph on ~60–70% of letters; spacing from the body, body-band kerning, word space ≥ 330. Rejected: uniform ears, thin spurs under the baseline.
- **Liquid-ink experimental display («Капля»)**: one design whose weights spread: metaball field (round-cone capsules per stroke, polynomial smooth-min, seeded noise) → marching squares → smoothing → cubic refit split at extrema. Free ends swell into bulbs, long strokes neck, ink pools toward the baseline; horizontals thinner than verticals (y-warped field) so counters survive. Stem 0.14/0.19/0.25/0.31 capH, melt and drip grow with weight, tracking goes negative so Black fuses into one stain; Light stays readable. Drips start only where ink meets the baseline (or a descender end), never from bars or empty space; no drips under round letters (reads as cedilla/ogonek); no detached dots between letters (read as periods/interpuncts); detached drops only directly under their own bead at heavy weights. Fallback rebuild with less spread when topology differs from a reference built at the same x-height. Digits: 6 = low bowl + hook as a quarter-ellipse arc ending at the top (open upper-right notch), 9 = rotated 6, so 6 ≠ 0 at 12–24 px cover size; no kerning table (a 12 u minimum gap would forbid the fusion); presets Капля, Чистая (melt 0, drip 0), Пятно, Дождь.
- **Modular keyhole display («Модуль»)**: every glyph is assembled from strokes/bands and MUST carry at least one rounding and at least one keyhole/slot (reference letters Ж А Ш О Н; punctuation: rounding only — round dots, pill ends; hyphens, dashes, quotes, « » and / carry no hole, ( ) ? ! & № keep one). Pinned stems stay within +8% of the normal stem — slim the keyhole instead of fattening the stem. Chevrons « » keep a guaranteed gap at Black (wider pitch, thinner arms).
- **Pixel grid display («Пиксель»)**: glyphs are recipes (rings, bars, stems, weight-keeping diagonals) re-rasterised per resolution (6–24 cells per cap) into exact grid cells merged into clean unions; a hinting ladder maps weight to [stem, bar] pixels and a per-resolution limit table (built from topology) caps it, so every slider combination keeps topology. Below ~6 rows a full charset cannot keep counters (%, &, 4). Lowercase Cyrillic = small-cap recipe at x-height; Latin m is arched by default, Cyrillic-style м = ss01 (swapped 15 Nov in `public/patches/latin-m.js`). Italic = whole-row shear, not a vector slant. Pixel serifs only on free stem ends with full side contact, never on bowls — stray 1–2-pixel islands read as periods (add an island check). Pixel shape/gap/stagger is applied at contour level and kerning is measured on the solid grid. Brush/eraser edits are stored per resolution and kept when resolution changes. 0 is a slashed zero by default and О stays a plain ring; ss01 = Cyrillic-style m + plain zero (O/0 IoU 0.58 Regular; Ш/Щ tail 2 cells deep with a left foot bar). Variable font: wght 300–900 only, one topology = the Regular cell map, weight as a rectilinear offset of the merged outline (point-compatible masters; clearance 0.34 cell; hhea = the static family 1010/−256).
- **Neo-grotesk text sans («Строка», Inter-class, user-approved)**: x-height 0.75 cap, ascender above cap, squircle ovals (kappa ≈ 0.60–0.62, elliptical corners, no stadium shapes), low contrast (hair/stem ≈ 0.89, ladder .94/.89/.85/.75 by weight), optical thinning where bowls/arches enter stems (b d p q h m n: straight stem, round form branching off with a smooth cleft — the approved junction style, applied to every Cyrillic and Latin letter). Signature terminal = the «б-cap»: a round form rises, bends in one wide corner into a short horizontal arm with a slanted cut; the same device flipped for bottom terminals (a c e s t l J j g y 2 3 5 6 9 С З Э Є). Never cut a round form with a slanted line on its descending shoulder. Ж/К: ink-trap slot with rounded tip in the stem/arm/leg pocket, diagonals optically thinned. R/Я leg one constant-width diagonal (no wedge). З waist = clean outer wedge notch; З round top vs 3 flat top. Л/Д vertical legs with rounded foot; Ц Щ short square tail under the right stem. m = 1.5 n advance with symmetric clefts; u = n rotated. ф/ю built from b/d bowls with thin crotches; ф ×1.14 width. 4: bar flush with the stem, no overhang; 3: smaller upper section. Fallback constructions for 2/3 under extreme pens to keep topology.
- **Renaissance antiqua («Ренесса», user-approved, ref = Renaissance Cyrillic caps)**: broad nib (default 7°, slider 3–16; live `core_renessa.js` and RECIPE_renessa, the earlier «≈16°» was the top of the range) with a separate thin pen (0.55 rx) for the thin diagonal; stem 82 / hair 25 (≈3.3:1; effective hair capped at 0.3 stem; hair slider 14–26; family hair ladder Light/Regular/Bold/Black = 19/25/28/28, stem 63/82/103/115, width +3 % Bold, +6 % Black); thin bracketed wedge serifs, sheared on diagonals; Λ-form Л Д in CAPS only (Д = Л + base with serifed descenders), lowercase л д classical (curved leg, foot serif); teardrop/beak terminals on C З S J c e s є (keep beaks light: vertical beaks close the c aperture at heavier hair); lining figures by default, oldstyle via onum; OpenType = figures, currency tabular forms, frac, ordn only (no smcp/c2sc, case, liga, ss01, locl are drawn). User-approved details: Э = mirrored С + wavy calligraphic bar with a small left flick; Ъ/ъ long left flag; б flag = tapered ribbon with slight upturn, weight ≈ r flag / c e terminals (not heavier); r arm = small ribbon; ф short ascender with left head wedge; Z z — two small wedge ticks at bar ends only, clean flush corners; 7 no overhang; 2 base ends in an upward wedge tick; Ц Щ ц щ tail = bottom bar continues right at baseline past the stem, ending in a small down wedge; & = classical single-stroke (upper loop leaning right, smooth oval lower bowl, clean crossing, free bracketed foot, light arm with wedge cut), advance ≈ 0.57 em. Cap colour balanced via widths (T Y У Л narrower-heavier, B В N M lighter) to 0.73–1.2 of H/O/N. Slider limits narrowed to what the stress sweep passes (nib 3–16, serif 28–52, bracket 0–40, hsl 14–36, ball 25–65, flare 0–50, stem slider 70–88); ( ) are curved arcs and “ ‘ are the 180° rotation of ” ’ (FIX_GLYPHS); Э at Black opens its aperture 0.4°/unit above stem 96 so the mirrored terminals never meet. VF axes wght/wdth/opsz (slnt dropped: sheared instances drifted from the static italic).
- **Cut-paper collage, child-like («Аппликация», user-approved, basic charset, unicase)**: each glyph = 1–4 paper pieces (4–9-vertex polygons, mostly straight scissor cuts, 1–2 soft curves on rounds, corners slightly blunt), unioned; per-letter recipes so constructions differ (Ж Ш М Ф wide, И Т Л narrow, О С Э one fat blob with a crooked counter, Е К Ш from separate strips). Personality seeded by glyph id: lean ±8°, height ±10 %, width ±12 %, baseline ±5 %, stem ratio inside a letter up to 1.6:1, bars at random heights, one leg longer. Lowercase = same forms + own cuts for frequent letters. Approved defaults: clean scissors (tear 0; «Рваный край» is a level, not a separate font), glued pieces (gaps 0, adjustable), looser spacing (sb ≈ 92). Own sliders: количество кусочков, острота резов, мягкие резы, нахлёст, разброс размера/наклона, скачет строка, Вариант (changes construction, not just jitter). Traps: a first round that is too tame reads as a rough grotesk — push individuality before showing; the Ш/Щ/Ц base bar must stay between the stems (an overhang reads as underlining in words); strips must start inside a wall, not in a counter (pinholes); no VF (pieces don't interpolate). Lowercase is capitals at 0.86 capH, clamped after the build for every seed (lean, scatter and jitter had inflated х ш я ф ж); seed 0 is a legal but lively draw. Topology guard compares with a reference built at the same seed and falls back by pulling sliders to defaults, then reducing stem. NBSP, thin space and NNBSP glyphs were reported missing for this font — verify against QA 24.
- **Constructor-ligature condensed display («Сцепка», user-approved, basic charset)**: ultra-heavy condensed rounded-square letters; ONE corner system for every glyph and ligature (build sharp → union → round once: convex R = 0.45·S like О, concave 0.5·R; diagonals end flush with stems or tiny arcs appear). Block-vertical forms approved by the user: К = stem + short mid stub + near-vertical arms (12–16°); У = Ч-top + U-cup bottom split by one slit; Ж = three verticals with inward wedge spurs merged into the centre stem (one contour); Й = solid triangle fused to stem and cap line, one wide slit (≈1.15 g) to the diagonal; Д block like П/Ц with stem-thick feet; Ц/Щ tails as thick as the bottom bar; Л legs equal perpendicular thickness. Ligature devices that read (explicit per-pair modes): roof (arm of Г/Т/Р continues over a partner ≥ 0.72 cap — the user's favourite: ГО РО ГА РА), true join (Е arm + Т roof = one bar), shared stem, step-nest only for К/Х pockets. Rejected: partner shrunk below 0.72 cap (reads as degree sign), foot-under bars (read as underline), НИ МЕ СТ ПН (merge into other letters), near-touch states. Rules: one slit g = inter-letter gap = roof slit; every gap is ≥ g or 0 (gapcheck); default calt = safe caps set, ss02 = dense + lowercase, ss01 = off-switch applied first; misread gate per ligature vs nearest plain pair (≥ 8 %) and nearest single letter (≥ 20 %) — in the repo the tools are `tools/iou_pair.py` + `stress_stsepka.js`, there are no scripts named gapcheck/misread gate (FIX_GLYPHS); preview shaper must emulate GSUB. After the P1 fixes the lookups hold calt 33 pairs and ss02 50 entries (the Latin pair HA and the МИН triple removed: H lost its right stem and read as KAMMER, the letters merged); lowercase stem = 0.86 × capital stem, clamp 0.27 xh. О = 0 stay twins by design. No VF.
- **Condensed tall display grotesque («Столб», id `stolb`, engine `cond/`, user-approved, basic charset)**: stsepka pipeline (pieces → raster 2 px/u → holes → trace → DP 2.2 px → 0.5 u lattice → fillet → cubic); built from the medians of 8 poster samples (stem/cap .109; lead review set the default .122–.128; ink widths ≈ 1.11 × the poster so counters stay readable: H .266, O .31, E .216). Cap 700, xh 525, asc 730, desc 170; stem 88 (Light 62, Bold 124, Black 158); registered round corners only (bowl R = rnd·1.1·S, straight corners nearly sharp); horizontals T = S(1 − 0.30·ctr), default ctr 35 %; one counter width cx = max(22, .26 S) between all stems, glyph width = n·S + (n−1)·cx; diagonals 1.03 × S perpendicular; no ink traps, no stencil, no extreme compression. Presets Плакат, Плотный, Мягкий, Строгий, Афиша, Журнал (the «Афиша» preset: stem 124, wdth 92, xh 540, urnd 40, uctr 20, uap 90, ugap 22). Owner rules: section 18b.
- **Stretch monoline caps («Тяга», user-approved)**: geometric minimalist monoline, CAPS ONLY (lowercase code points draw the same letters); only straight horizontal segments elongate, stems, round ends, bowls and counters never change. Stroke S = 0.1875 H (131 u at cap 700), round strokes S × 1.08, letter gap 0.135 H, word space 0.36 H + gap. Classes: FREE (k 1…6, steps ×1.5 ×2 ×3 ×4 ×6 = alternates .s1….s5), DIAGONAL (k 1…2, steps ×1.5 ×2, then lock), HORIZONTAL (₽ # up to ×2), NONE (@ * © ° • · \ | І . , : ; ! ? « » „ “ ” ‘ ’ " ' … ( ) / & № % 1). Stretch is typed as `letter~…` (one GSUB type-4 ligature lookup registered in calt, liga and clig: Figma applies calt, Word needs «Стандартные лигатуры»), made in the justified «Блок» tool (per line, one best candidate first, then water-fill weighted by score × width; typed and dragged letters are locked) or dragged by the right edge. Kerning by classes of canonical glyphs, every pair clamped by the real nearest gap of every member and stretched alternate (21 distinct values 14…110 u, none above 15 % UPM). Own sliders: tsw 12–23 (the only stroke width), trd 0–100, tbs 80–130, tap 4–30, tst 1–6, tlg (block only). Symbols @ # * © ₽ ° • · \ | follow the same logic (stroke S, butt ends, half-ring rounds).
- **Faceted swash caps («Кортес», user-approved, caps only)**: heavy chiselled display caps; stems vertical and thick (0.195 H = 137 u), horizontals thin (hair ≈ 0.35–0.4 stem), the counter is a LENS leaning top-left → bottom-right while outer shapes stay near-symmetric; ONE wedge family for every serif, beak, bracket triangle and arm end (flat-topped 44° wedge, straight faces, no bracket, sharp tips); S 2 3 5 З J 6 9 are elliptical-nib strokes (22–26°) with wedge spurs; glyph = painter-order polygons (add/subtract) with a raster-trace fallback and a Schneider refit (22 of 162 glyphs). Neighbouring serifs almost touch (gap ≈ 0.03–0.05 H). Sliders kwt 118–158, kct 20–70, ksr 25–75, ksw 30–75, kwd 92–112, kap −20…60. Й breve is a V wedge in the serif facet family; the Latin breve stays a crescent. ss02 = superior letters triggered by `^`.
- **Hand-line monoline («Нить», user-approved, frozen)**: one thin round-pen line IS the letter; each glyph is 1–6 loose skeleton strokes that meet like a felt-tip. Seeded hand from hash(char | seed), independent of neighbours and sliders: per-stroke tilt, low-frequency wobble, radial wobble on ovals, width ±8 %, glyph lean ±4.5°, height ±6.5 %, baseline ±3.4 %, width ±7 %. Round pen rx = ry = S/2 through the host skeleton engine and an exact union; holes below (1.2 S)² are filled (slivers between near-parallel strokes). Cap 740, xh 480, asc 900, desc 260; lowercase Cyrillic = wider (1.18 ×), looser small caps of the same hand; Latin lowercase has real forms. One thickness slider `nlw` 2–80 (S = 740·(0.022 + 0.09·nlw/100)) drives everything; family weights are computed from the user's value (Light ×0.70, Bold ×1.39, Black ×1.68). A safety net rebuilds a glyph that changed parts or holes (calmer wobble, narrower, thinner, finally the reference hand); default seed never triggers it.
- **Connecting brush script from a reference («Кисть», waiting for materials, owner's method of 12 Nov 2026)**: (1) build a wide brush with a soft start and end that imitates the brush of the original, size and angle from stroke measurements of the reference; (2) analyse every letter of the reference as Bézier skeletons — centre lines only, points and handles in writing order, saved as overlays on the reference to study rhythm, construction, joins and loop size; contours are never copied; (3) apply the brush to the skeletons with the angle tuned per stroke (pressure on downstrokes, thin connections, brush turn on rounds); (4) letters missing from the reference are built in the same logic and rhythm. Measured on the months reference: slant ≈ 20°, lowercase 0.57 of cap height, pointed brush, thin stroke ≈ 0.4–0.45 of the thick one (first fit 94 / 42 u = 0.45; later pass thick ≈ 0.2 xh, thin ≈ 0.12 xh), joins rise from below at ≈ 50°, high exits after о в б. Contextual variants by neighbours; a «Соединения» panel with height and tension sliders for the whole font and entry / exit / tail switches per glyph, saved in the project. Failures to avoid: a brush too thick and round closes counters (е н к ю became blobs) and leaves a drop at every stroke start (a taper is needed); a loop copied onto every capital (М И Н read as Я); tails at word end running into the next word; straight connections and angular bowls where the reference has arcs. Checks: QA 34.
- **Module-grid roman capital (Karpenko Figs 40–43)**: square grid, module m = cap/10, stem = 1 m; serif bracket = compass circle r = 1 m tangent to stem and baseline/cap line (centre 1 m out, 1 m in); serif hairline 0.15–0.2 m; foot = stem + 1 m each side. R: bowl to ≈ 0.49 H, two bowl centres ≈ 0.7 m apart, bowl thick ≈ 0.9 m / hairlines ≈ 0.4 m, concave leg from the bowl underside ≈ 1.3 m tapering to a hairline. U: bowl = concentric circles r ≈ 4 m / 3 m. Compare apex, crossbar joint and foot across genres.
- **Kit-of-parts modular Cyrillic (Karpenko «НИКС», Bauhaus kit)**: rectangles, quarter-circles, half-discs, round-capped bars; every corner either square or one radius family (r = 1 m or 2 m); stroke ≈ 0.15–0.2 H; Ф from two half-circles + stem, Х from quarter arcs, Л quarter-round shoulder, Д feet curling inward with round ends, Ж asterisk of round-capped bars; ship wide and condensed alternates (Н Ж).
- **Single-device display generators**: crescent letters (each stroke = lune of two equal circles offset 40–50 % of max stroke, pointed ends); ball-terminal monoline (circle/bar skeleton, ball 1.5–2× stroke); cleaved heavy letters (one thin diagonal slit through the heaviest stroke); pillow letters (ultra-heavy rounded + white inline + outer outline); bubble/blob unicase (Ф disc + bar, Е З as half-moons).
- **Vyaz**: column rhythm stem : gap = 1 : 1, letters 3.5–4× stem pitch tall, neighbours share masts, round letters (О С Е Я) become almond lenses between stems, small letters nest in notches of tall ones, spiral curls fill voids, monoline ≈ 1:1; Church-Slavonic display faces add flared spurs and extreme height (Psaltyr, FitaVjaz).
- **Display/unique layer**: change parameters, signature forms via stylistic sets — never break the base construction or user rules.

## 11b. Generator vocabulary (from the specimen surveys of Shpakovsky's catalogue; the full survey note SPECIMENS_SHPAKOVSKY_KB.md is not in the v94 archive, this section is its only copy)
- **Construction switches**: roundness model (rect + oval / square + circle / rounded rectangle / octagonal chamfer, one per font); contrast model (none, sans modulation, broad nib at angle, pointed-pen pressure, vertical didone); terminal family (flat, angled, ball, teardrop, beak, flare, wedge, round cap); serif family (none, hairline, bracketed wedge, slab, Tuscan notch, flared); case model (normal, lowercase = small caps, unicase).

## 12. Reference-driven design (user supplies images)
- Extract genre-defining traits first and list them as acceptance criteria. Build ORIGINAL letterforms; historic public-domain models may be followed more closely.
- Study copy (only when the owner asks): copy an open-licence font up to about 95 % of its form, changing only overall proportions and stroke widths, to learn its construction. The copy stays a study file; library fonts remain original.
- The owner's red sketch on a screenshot is the spec: follow it exactly, and if the result overshoots, return to the sketch instead of reinterpreting it.
- First deliverable: side-by-side sheet of key letters. Colour first, details second.
- Ornaments read as part of the stroke silhouette; vary them by letter structure.
- Design missing letters from the same modules/pen, then check them in words.
- After two non-converging rounds, show the user 2–3 concrete directions.
- Approval pack every round: specimen (full set), vs_ref (reference beside ours), text proofs (60/28/18/14 px), serifs/spurs at ≥400 px, weights, and roundN_compare (previous vs current, changed glyphs large). The lead looks at every sheet before sending; ship nothing the lead has not viewed.
- User sketches (marks drawn over our render): crop each glyph at full resolution and place our render at the SAME size beside or over the sketch; match the drawn shape — neither more timid (changes invisible at specimen size) nor exaggerated (the user calls it «перестарался»). Ticks/checkmarks = approved, freeze those glyphs. Ask only when a mark is ambiguous (e.g. which letter an item refers to).
- When a sketch is ambiguous or its literal geometry fails readability, render 2–3 variants on one decision sheet (big + in words, next to the confusable letter) and let the user pick, instead of silently deviating.
- When a redraw gets worse, revert to the last version the user liked and fix only the part they marked.
- Record every approved remark (glyph rules, proportions) in memory/recipe so it carries to future fonts; a new font is shown for approval before integration; large features go through staged approval (concept → detailed mock → interactive prototype → production).
- Reference tracing is for study only: take centre lines and measurements, never copy the contour; keep the skeletons as overlays on the reference so the method can be checked (brush script, 12 Nov 2026).
- Before showing a reference-driven letter, list the parts of the reference glyph and tick each one (к arm-end hook, б long top flag, ж spurs): the owner rejected a pass because parts of the letters were not drawn at all.
- Fit by an objective measure when the target is a reference image (overlap of painted area per letter, word-width ratio, QA 34); an eyeballed «too high contrast» was wrong in Афиша and its fix was a regression — measure first (section 16h lesson 1).
- Long agent sessions degrade (past ~400k tokens): hand the hard glyphs to a fresh agent with numeric specs and a saved state file; give every pass a measurable goal.

## 13. User-drawn fonts (draw-your-font tools)
- Grids: modular rectangles (2×4, 3×4, 4×4 cells), circles + diagonals, 4×4 tile, 45° diagonal, pixel; snap to nodes, quadrants, intersections; one-click cell/triangle/quarter-disc fills.
- Every tool outputs filled closed contours (strokes outlined + simplified; eraser = subtract).
- Полиграмма: overlay drawn letters of one construction family as thin tinted outlines (no fills) and offer editable drafts (П←Н, Ш/Ц/Щ←Н·П·Ш, Э←С mirrored + bar, Ь←Р, Ъ←Ь + flag, Ы←Ь + stem, Е←Н/Г).
- Anchors: automatic top/bottom anchors, draggable; marks drawn once (кратка with thick ends, diaeresis, acute, grave, circumflex, caron, macron, tilde, ring, cedilla, ogonek, dot) generate Й Ё Ў Ї and accented Latin automatically, updating live; detachable.
- Assistant: stem consistency, heights/baseline/overshoot, optical sidebearings, specks, open paths, shared forms, oversized marks, languages covered + letters to draw next — each with an automatic fix where possible.
- Test as a REAL in-memory font (FontFace) before saving; persist sidebearing adjustments.
- Cell-grid drawing (Inbetween-style, `kb/CELL_GRID_KB.md`): store cells + grid settings as data per glyph, regenerate ink; fusion = cells ∪ bridges (band = cell's own extent across the bridge) then close concave corners only — convex parts keep full cell-shape curvature (lone circle = circle, stroke end = semicircle); shape A→B morph %, rotation, cols/rows, subgrid, link scope (glyph / grids / all settings); settings change = animated SDF blend (instant under reduced motion); coarse preview during drag, exact contours after the gesture.
  - Rows map the cap height (700); «Нижние ряды» 0–4 add rows below the baseline. Bridge band = the shape's extent perpendicular to the bridge (asymmetric for triangle and pentagon); at «Скругление стыков» 0 the band is capped to one cell so the shape texture shows. Layers (Inbetween «Calque»): each layer has its own grid settings, cells, visibility and name; ink = union of the visible layers, each fused and cached separately; hidden layers are excluded from the font (say so in the UI); links work by layer index.
  - Mirror lesson: every cell shape is symmetric about the vertical axis, so an exact mirror is a rotation of −θ (horizontal) or 180° − θ (vertical); negating θ alone is wrong for asymmetric shapes. Known gap: a triangle or pentagon always points up before rotation.
  - QA of the tool (`tests/e2e_cells.py` in the archive suite): no self-intersections, node limits, capsule/circle deviation < 1 u, six shapes × five rotations with straight runs spread < 1.5 u, panel = canvas state, persistence, TTF contours. Lessons: the panel re-renders from the model on every settings change (stale chips showed «Круг» over a hex grid); screenshot scaling can fake a «small radius» — measure curvature numerically before changing geometry; key 7 belongs to glyph «7» (tool keys 1–6, «Ячейки» = `=`/`+`).

## 14. Professional type tester (conventions)
- Size as кегль in pt with mm (1 pt = 0.3528 mm) and px readout; интерлиньяж in pt and %; трекинг in 1/1000 em; выключка left/centre/right/justified + hyphenation; формат набора in characters (optimum 60–70, acceptable 45–75); never clip text — scale the preview and show the scale.
- Panels: Набор, Различимость (confusable pairs at 12 px), Цветность (grey evenness, rivers), Водопад (8–72 pt).
- Typesetting rules to support/check: выключка влево · вправо · по центру · по ширине · по формату (+ произвольная); висячая строка = концевая ≤ 4 знаков; коридор = совпадение пробелов в 3+ строках; втяжка = 1 em; отбивка и отступ не вместе; трекинг: разрядка для крупного кегля и выворотки; выделения от сдержанного к сильному: курсив → капитель → прописные → полужирное → кегль; never fake small caps or stretched letters.
- More typesetting checks (`kb/TYPO_KARPENKO_KB.md`): висячая строка = концевая строка ≤ 4 знаков, «сироты» = 1–2 строки вверху или внизу колонки; tracking range −100…+100 per 1000; mix only contrasting faces (антиква + гротеск, never two similar serifs); classic margins: outer wider than inner, top narrower than bottom; three-level hierarchy (title, subtitle, text); flag setting is one-edge justification, «косынка» is centred with narrowing lines.
- Pangrams RU: «В чащах юга жил-был цитрус… — да, но фальшивый экземпляръ», «Съешь ещё этих мягких французских булок, да выпей же чаю», «Экс-граф? Плюш изъят. Бьём чуждый цен хвощ!», «Южно-эфиопский грач увёл мышь за хобот на съезд ящериц».

## 15. Font metadata and library tags
- One metadata entry per font drives covers, hover cards and search: id, name, year, desc (1–2 sentences in ParaType terms), tags[], sample, charset, styles, weights; a test fails if year/desc/tags are missing. Covers: «Название + год», sized by cap height.
- Tag facets: Группа (classification), Инструмент/конструкция, Пропорции, Контраст, Назначение (Текстовые/Акцидентные), Набор знаков, Языки, Технологии (Вариативный), Экспериментальные, Пиксельные. Tag «Вариативный» on every font that exports a VF; «Широкие» by real proportions (or the wide end of the family). Wrong labels seen: «Шрифт пера» (not a category → «Рукописные» + tool), «Вязь» for an ustav-based face.
- Live vocabulary (`service/FONT_META.md`, `LIB_TAGS` in `ui_lib_u1.js`): facets `class` (Гротески, Новые гротески, Геометрические, Антиква, Ренессанс, Рукописные, Готические, Древнерусские, Унциальные), `tool` (Ширококонечное перо, Остроконечное перо, Связные, Ротунда, Устав, Модульные), `prop` (Узкие, Нормальные, Широкие), `contrast` (Неконтрастные), `purpose` (Текстовые, Акцидентные), `charset` (Базовый набор, Кириллица + латиница), `feature` (Вариативный); ids are stable, labels may change, `TAG_PARENT` makes a sub-group part of its group (ngrot, geo → grot; rot → gothic; ustav → old). Card and list rows show up to three tags of the first five facets. Required record per font: year, desc, tags ≥ 1; everything else (charset, glyph count, styles, weights) is derived from the font itself so it cannot go stale. Fonts without lowercase (Модуль, Рывок) draw their cover from the caps; cover size is set by cap height, not string width.
- Narrow fonts may get a taller cover than the rest. Reference-based fonts ship with the basic charset unless the owner asks for the full one.
- Tag by the measured proportion, not by the wish (m29 audit item 18): Kvadra carried «Нормальные» while Н/cap was .98 → 1.15 (≥ 100 % = «Широкие») and Rotunda was described as wide but untagged — both now carry `wide`; Устав Black leaves the 40–60 % «Узкие» band and Модуль .60 is untagged (open).

## 16b. Karpenko digest («Шрифт и типографика», 2025) — full notes in kb/TYPO_KARPENKO_KB.md
- 6 stroke groups: I sans no contrast · II sans with flared ends · III moderate contrast + serifs · IV contrast + serifs · V low contrast + serifs · VI low contrast + heavy serifs.
- Contrast: Roman capitals and old style 1:2–1:3 (axis ≈ 25–30° — general knowledge, the book only says the axis straightens later), transitional 1:4–1:7 (taller, sharper serifs, smaller bracket radius, vertical O axis), modern — hairline unbracketed serifs, vertical axes, round ball terminals.
- Units: квадрат = 48 pt = 18,048 мм; агат 5,5 pt; em = кегль; en = ½ em; длинное тире = em, короткое = en; литера 0,918″; text 5–12/14 pt, display 16–72 pt; children: ≥ 14 pt (pre-school), 10–12 (primary), 8–12 (teen).
- Small caps ≈ caps −25 % height, slightly wider, lowercase weight. Glyph ≠ grapheme. Font evaluation: нейтральность, прозрачность (сочетаемость), ёмкость, удобочитаемость.
- Calligraphy: дукт = nib width/letter height + nib angle + stroke order; one stroke = one movement. Vyaz: mast ligatures (shared stem of two letters), half-stems, voids filled with shrunken almond О Е С; round vs angular (штамбованная).
- Pixel/8-bit: aliased modular letters dictated by screen resolution; «пиксель-арт» as a style.

## 16c. Shpakovsky digest («Шрифты. Справочное пособие дизайнера», 2006) — the note file TYPO_SHPAKOVSKY_KB.md is not in the v94 archive; this digest is its only copy
- **Weight by counter ratio** (lowercase): light = stem 2–3.5× thinner than the counter; demibold = stem ≤ counter; bold = stem > counter. Use as a sanity check on weight ladders.
- **Frutiger indices from H** (bands overlap; build H first and read weight/width from it). Weight d/H (stem/cap height): Thin < 5 %, UltraLight 4–8, ExtraLight 6–10, Light 9–13, Book/Regular 12–17, Demi 16–22, Bold 20–27, ExtraBold 23–30, Black > 28 %. Width L/H (distance between H stem axes / cap height): ExtraCondensed < 45 %, Condensed 40–60, Normal 56–80, Extended 74–110, ExtraExtended > 100 %. Report a font's d/H and L/H next to its named weights.
- **Proportion and contrast scales**: normal width/height ≈ 3/4, narrow ≈ 1/2–2/3, wide > 1; contrast (horizontal/vertical) 1:1 none · 4:5 normal · 1:2 medium (higher = contrasting).
- **Genre traits**: old-style antiqua (broad pen, moderate contrast, bracketed serifs, inclined axes; Venetian → Italian-French → Dutch → English) → transitional (pen + engraving) → new style (strong contrast, vertical axes, long hairline serifs) — contrast rises, serifs sharpen, axes straighten over time. Slab: heavy rectangular serifs, low contrast, large x-height (Egyptian, geometric, humanist, Clarendon = rounded slab). Grotesques: old (1816, crude, semi-closed), new (1950s Swiss, closed, ascender ≈ cap height, many weights), geometric (1930s, circle/square/triangle, open), humanist (old-style structure, open, slight contrast, varied widths, smaller x-height); antiqua-grotesque = sans with micro-serifs/flares. Oblique slant: small in geometric sans, medium–large in new grotesques. Blackletter: textura (narrow, double break, rhombic ends), rotunda (wide, single break), schwabacher, fraktur (two breaks, forked ascenders). Semi-ustav: basis of the first Cyrillic type, less contrast than ustav, long extenders, caps sitting below the lowercase baseline.
- **Legibility**: familiarity dominates; moderate contrast beats high (о н п и л differ mainly in hairlines that vanish on matte paper), but zero contrast reads monotonously; short serifs beat long; children/beginners read low-contrast sans better; lowercase н ≈ as wide as tall — strong condensing closes counters; every letter distinct yet unified (geometry, widths, terminals, extenders). Lowercase sets darker/denser than caps.
- **Optical corrections listed**: round/pointed letters (3 0 C Э J a б e, A V W N) overshoot; squares and circles look stretched — correct; diagonals and horizontals look heavier than verticals — thin them; diagonal–vertical and diagonal–diagonal junctions look heavy — notch/cut, more so at large sizes; curves look thinner than straights — thicken slightly (В has deliberately unequal stems).
- **Print tolerance and economy**: moderate/no contrast, short serifs and wide counters survive printing; long near-touching serifs and narrow counters fill in; very thin hairlines drop out. Do not save space with condensed text faces — reduce size of a clear face instead.
- **Optical-size bands** (OpenType opsz anchors): caption 6–8 pt, text 9–13, subhead 14–24, display 25–72. Specimen convention: text faces at 10/11 pt, display at 24 pt.
- **Point systems and size names**: Didot point 0.376065 mm (1/72 French inch 27.077 mm), Anglo-American 0.3528 mm, pica 12 pt = 4.23 mm; 6 нонпарель, 7 миньон, 8 петит, 9 боргес, 10 корпус, 12 цицеро, 14 миттель, 16 терция, 18 парагон, 20 текст, 24 двойной цицеро, 48 квадрат. Kerning scales with size; tracking runs inverse to size (looser in 8–12 pt text, tighter in display).
- **Contour quality checklist**: lost smoothness at primitive joins is the most common error; on-curve nodes at every extreme; inner angles < 20° get a tiny 1–3 u segment instead of a needle point; no overlong curves, no curves with two inflections or loops, no wildly unequal control vectors; true verticals/horizontals exact (1 u deviation over 500 u shows as jaggies and stem drift); baseline/overshoot inconsistencies multiply into a staircase in text. Test glyphs for Cyrillic contour checks: а Д з К.
- **Family and naming hygiene**: ship real italic/bold (no synthetic styles) and complete RIBBI linking; unique PostScript names without spaces, identical across versions only when outlines are identical (same-name different-version fonts reflow documents); rename modified fonts. Classification fields for export: MS family (Roman/Swiss/Modern/Script/Decorative), IBM class (sFamilyClass), PANOSE (Times = 2 2 6 3 5 4 5 2 3 4).
- **Decorative variant system** (catalogue practice, e.g. Arsenal families): one base skeleton expanded by effect variants — titling caps, outline, inline, shadow (offset direction up/down), 3D/extruded, two-tone, rounded corners, double line, stripes, rough/distressed, broken, grain. Model it as effect parameters on one skeleton (cf. section 9 decorative styles and 11b), each variant passing the same topology checks.

## 16d. Shashkina digest («Шрифт в практике дизайна», 2011) — TYPO_SHASHKINA_KB.md is not in the v94 archive; this digest is its only copy (the book scan stays in the owner's archive, not copied)
- Two construction principles, one per font: rectangle (equal-width rectangles, oval rounds) vs square (circle О, strictly varying widths: В < Н П < О); a compass-circle О forbids oval В Ю.
- Identity = stroke ratio + roundness model + axis; weight/width/slant/size are variations.
- Cyrillic construction groups: Н Г Е П Т Ц Щ Ш (straight) · Л Д М И (vertical + diagonal) · А У Х (diagonal) · О С Э З (round) · Ж Б В К Р Ф Ч Ы Ь Ъ Ю Я (mixed, largest). Д К М У Х carry individual forms that steer the rest.
- Polygram: caps Н → И П Ц (+ parts of Д Л М У, Б В Е К Р Ю Я); О → С Э Ю; А → Д Л М variants, Х У; Е → Г Т; В → З Ы Ь Ф Я; К → Ж; lowercase н → а в г д и к л п т ч ц ь я; о → б е з р с ф э ю. Implement as inheritance so one change propagates.
- Module grid: e.g. 4:5 cells, stem = 1 module, start with Н and О; Ж М Щ Ш Ю (Ы) ≈ 1.5× narrow letters. Pen angles: Roman capital 25–30°, rustic 60°, uncial 0° (М Ж Я change angle).
- Weight by stem:counter: light 1:6–1:4, semibold 1:2, bold 1:1. Symmetry classes for spacing: open-right Б В Г Е К Р С Ы Ю; open-left З У Ч Э Я.
- Visibility: smallest element d = distance × 0.000291; minimum letter H = 5d, width 3d; optimum ×2. Small sizes: larger x-height, wider, weak contrast (~0.75), open forms, no fragile detail.
- Figure–ground personas (Favorsky): classical (spring arcs, rounded brackets, object-like), romantic/didone (compressed verticals, hairline serifs, spatial), gothic (lattice), heavy poster grotesk (flat), thin grotesk (contour), handwriting (vibrating edge). A glyph may be distorted only until it stops reading as its grapheme.

## 16e. Bannikova digest (typejournal.ru «Галина Банникова») — kb/TYPO_BANNIKOVA_KB.md
- Classify and build by SKELETON, not serif: circle-based (low contrast, gradual thickening, bracketed serifs, drop terminals → old-style) vs vertical-based (→ modern/didone). Contrast, serifs and terminals must follow from the chosen skeleton.
- Width variation (разноширинность) is the virtue of the Russian civil type and was proven to raise legibility (1951 study, Банниковская ranked 1st). Rhythm is the main style-forming factor. Avoid the «частокол»: slanted descenders on д ц щ (not copies of the т г serifs), varied terminal types (drops on а к л ≠ terminals on с ж), ж not as two mirrored к, б with a shared spine and a rigid bowl joint.
- Judge each glyph as one black/white unit by eye; draw roman+italic and Cyrillic+Latin together in words; re-proportion Latin instead of copying Cyrillic.
- Optical gain hits narrow letters hardest: open their counters or lower their contrast.
- High-contrast faces for small sizes (Байконур): entasis, squarish inner counters, short massive serifs at a slight angle, thickened joins for constructional unity.
- Historical forms (Petrine Р У Ф, old-style figures) belong in stylistic sets, not as defaults.
- Width logic of the capitals follows the skeleton: if О is a circle a NARROW Р fits it; small-radius bowls give narrow letters (В), large radii wide ones (О), so old-style cap widths are naturally uneven.
- Defects she named to AVOID: monotonous rhythm, weak stroke modulation, mechanical skeleton, dry uniform drawing; accented letters of the national alphabets are sketched together with the base set, and unsuitable historical italic forms (Lomonosov А, slanted п for в) are rejected by legibility.
- Full notes and the rule list: `kb/TYPO_BANNIKOVA_KB.md`.

## 16f. Construction by geometry and points (video study) — TYPO_CONSTRUCTION_VIDEO_KB.md is not in the v94 archive; this digest is its only copy
- The reel is a promo, not a tutorial: no grid lecture, only node/handle overlays on G, a, K, R, O, e, i and weight morphs. Use it for node grammar, not for proportions.
- Node grammar: on-curve nodes of round strokes ONLY at extrema (O = 4 per contour, outer and inner); handles at extrema strictly H/V; corner nodes (no handles) only on flat cuts, serifs, K-type polygons; tangent nodes where a curve leaves a straight stem/hairline (handle collinear with the straight edge, vertical on stems, horizontal on hairlines).
- Handle length = k × distance to the neighbouring node, k 0.55–0.65, NOT symmetric when neighbours differ (G top node ≈200 / ≈100). Measured on Regular O: outer top 0.55, outer side 0.60, counter 0.61–0.66 (counters fuller so they stay open).
- Terminal arcs: one extra smooth shoulder node at ≈45° (handles ≈74/49/53), terminal = flat cut of 2 corners; terminal tip x aligns with the stem edge (±2 units); left/right round extremum exactly at mid-height; same ~2 % overshoot for all rounds.
- Stress: tilt is made by shifting inner extremum nodes sideways (offset ≈ t·tan θ, θ 0–3°, shown 2.8°), handles stay H/V; contrast is a weight-dependent parameter (Regular hair/side ≈ 1.07, Black strongly contrasted).
- Consistency: one ordered node list for every weight (interpolation-ready), alternates (ss01: a, k, R) = same skeleton with one feature swapped, one handle-ratio family, shared alignment columns.
- Automatable QA: off-extremum smooth nodes = 0 (except shoulder nodes); handle angle at extrema <0.5° from H/V; k in [0.5,0.7] with low variance across o/c/e/d/b/p/q; same stress angle across round glyphs; node count/order identical across weights; corner nodes only at cuts; terminal-to-stem x alignment.
- Generator: ring/ellipse with separate outer/inner kappa; tilt param on inner extrema; junction nodes [x,y,deg,hIn,hOut] with forced deg 0/90; build all weights from the same node list.

## 16h. Pro display-serif outline rules (Yeseva One, Playfair 700/900, Prata; measured) — `kb/TYPO_PRO_OUTLINES_KB.md` (the anatomy_*.png and profiles.png schemes stay in the owner's archive)
Normalise to S = n stem, X = x-height. Check every new serif face against these BEFORE showing the user.
- Hair is absolute: 20u ring hair, 26–32u thin diagonals, serif tips 15–21u — at every weight.
- Ring: side 1.05 S (1.04–1.10), thick plateau to 30° from the side, then fall (u = 1,.99,.95,.79,.51,.22,0 at 0…90°); outer kappa 0.60–0.71 (squarer than a circle), counter = lens (top handle 0.95, side 0.22).
- Arch: shoulder = ONE cubic landing on the right stem at 0.70X (not 0.50X — a half-ring glued to stems looks amateur); outer notch down the stem to 0.85X (26–34° sliver); inner crotch 0.75X, tangent join; thin point left of centre (~108°), right shoulder full S by 45°.
- Serifs: concave bracket cubic (tip handle 0.81–0.88 L, stem handle 0.61–0.68 rise), or a straight wedge — pick one school per face. Flag tops flat in pros (a slope must be a deliberate reference trait).
- Balls 0.72–1.03 S with a 12–24u neck; e bar centre 0.61X; v/w/y apex pointed (≤0.6 S); k arms 0.3–0.45 S (not hairline).
- Bowl hairlines run at ~20–25u to 0.35 S from the stem, then a sharp inside corner.
- Node budgets of the pro faces: o = 4 + 4 nodes (Prata 4 + 6), n 22–26, e 12–17, a 20–31, r / y 18–29; every smooth node of a bowl, arch or shoulder sits on an extreme (tangent H or V), off-extreme smooth nodes only at diagonal-to-curve transitions; everything else is a corner (serif tips, stem/serif meets, notch, crotch, terminal cuts); a terminal cut is two corner nodes 0.3–1.0 S apart with no handles on the cut. A shoulder is ONE cubic from the top extreme to the stem tangent, never a full quarter-ellipse.
- Stroke profile numbers: side : hair contrast ≈ 7.5:1 (Y 8.5, P7 7.6, P9 9.4, Pr 5); axis exactly vertical, mirror-symmetric ring, o overshoot 14–15 u; overhang of a serif is weight-independent, 56–70 u = 0.11–0.135 X, tip 15–21 u; stem pitch / X = 0.58–0.68 for n h u; thick diagonal = 1.00 S perpendicular, thin diagonal 0.18 S (26–32 u) and 3–7° steeper than the thick one; v apex width at 0.04 X = 0.36–0.62 S (a flat 1.27 S apex looked wrong), v / y baseline overshoot −0.004…−0.04 X; ball neck 12–17 u (Playfair), 24 u (Yeseva, Prata); e crossbar 19–25 u thick.
- c / e lower stroke has two schools — pick one: monotone taper (1.05 S at 200–220°, 0.67 at the bottom, 0.14 S at the terminal) or classic ring (hair at the bottom held to the terminal); never hair at the bottom and then a re-swell (that is the lump of lesson 3). A y tail is a hairline (0.11–0.14 S) over 20–65 % of its path ending in a ball 0.66–1.0 S, not a 1.0 S bucket.
- Foot bracket profile (rise fraction at 10/25/50/75/90 % of the overhang): Playfair .42/.20/.06/.01/0 (a fillet that hugs the base and then climbs), wedge faces .90/.75/.50/.25/.10 (linear). Playfair-type cubic from the tip: P0 = (0, t), P1 = (0.85 L, t) horizontal, P2 = (L, rise − 0.65·rise) vertical, P3 = (L, H); tip cut vertical, base bowed +4 u toward the stem, underside flat within 4 u. Head flag: tip = foot tip (0.12–0.15 S), top flat at X (0–3° slope), underside the same bracket.
- Cyrillic professional constructions (`kb/TYPO_PRO_OUTLINES_KB.md` section 9): б = o-type ellipse with a tall lens counter; the flag is ONE ribbon starting on the bowl's left outer edge at 0.60–0.65 X, rising on a concave curve to the ascender (≈ 1.50 X), turning right to a sheared, nearly horizontal end ≈ 0.25–0.3 X thick, thinning to a hair where it leaves the bowl; д = the printed form (flat roof at X with serifs, long concave left leg to a bracketed foot, right stem, feet dropping to −0.32 X, ≈ 0.12 X wide); ж arms are S-curves ending in a ball at x-height with tangent smooth joins 20–30 u; к arms follow the diagonal rule; э / з terminals follow the e / c terminals; у tail = y tail; ч bowl hair meets the stem at ≈ 0.55 X with a thin wedge.
- Lessons (Afisha 2, rounds 1–5, see kb/AFISHA_LESSONS_v2.md): (0) Balls, teardrops and ladle tails were rejected by the user as a family — when several terminals of one kind get crossed, replace the whole family with the face's approved terminal (here: horizontal-cut wedge), never patch one glyph. Crossed marks = rejected element, unmarked = frozen; freeze by outline XOR. Lowercase д in roman = printed or ∂ form; the g-like descending д reads as Latin g (дом → gom). Long-context agents degrade: give hard glyphs to a fresh agent with numeric specs; the lead tunes the last parameters himself when an agent says «can't» (y tail: end far left, not under the foot). (1) MEASURE before diagnosing — an eyeballed «contrast too high» was wrong and its fix (thicker hair, blobby terminals) was a regression; revert at once. (2) Patching glyphs one by one after user marks never converges; fix the shared primitive. (3) A terminal profile that goes hair → re-swell reads as a lump; keep profiles monotone. (4) Put the pro numbers into quality gates (ring profile, arch landing, notch, serif tip, node counts) so regressions are caught before the user sees them. (5) The lead views every sheet at ≈ 300 px and in words at 40–80 px before sending. (6) Rejected as a family in Афиша 2: balls and teardrops (r f j t л g), ladle/bucket tails (y у), leaf я legs, g-like lowercase д, trapezoid д, thin spike tails on ц щ, a squared «L-bracket» б flag, and the «thicker hairline + teardrop family» (v3, whose diagnosis was eyeballed; measured contrast 0.17–0.18 already matched).

## 18. Applying this skill to a project
- Short English project brief next to the code (metrics, parameters with safe ranges, components, user rules, test commands) plus a glossary digest file (kb/PARATYPE_KB.md style) and the latest font audit (kb/AUDIT_*.md with resolution status). Agents read the brief + this skill, not each other's reports.
- New font: brief → genre defaults (11c) → control glyphs → components → full set → unique layer → metadata entry (year, desc, tags) → passport/languages check.
- Edits: fix the component, re-check all dependants.
- Per-font proof hooks instead of loosening global checks; font-specific sliders hidden on other fonts.
- UI labels use ParaType terms first with a plain tooltip («Основные штрихи», «Соединительные штрихи», «Апертура», «Свисание», «Полуапроши», «Апекс»; weights «Светлое/Нормальное/Жирное/Сверхжирное»).
- One source of truth per font file; delete stale split copies.
- Read order for a task on an existing font: this skill → the font's entry in section 18b → `service/RECIPE_<font>.md` (its later sections win over its header; RECIPE_kortes.md still says DRAFT but W86 integrates it) → the matching `kb/` file from section 19. When a recipe and the live code disagree, the code wins and the recipe is corrected (RECIPE_ryvok.md still lists the v3 stem table .28/.315/.345/.375; the live ladder is .18/.245/.33/.38).
- A new font goes to the owner for approval (key letters, both themes) before it is built into the service; send short interim reports while it is in progress. After two rejected rounds start the font again, delete the old versions and keep the lessons.
- Owner terminology: «прописная» in his remarks often means lowercase and «заглавная» means capital; when a remark says «прописные» without naming letters, check both cases.
- Contextual brush variants («Кисть», connected scripts): up to 5 variants per letter, off by default, only by the user's choice; connections adjustable per font and per glyph.
- After each font or genre: write a 10–20 line recipe (what worked, traps, rejected versions) and fold it into section 11.
- Parameter robustness by narrowing, not widening: when a random/extreme sweep breaks topology, first fix geometry adaptively, then narrow that font's slider limits to the range the sweep passes; fast per-glyph sweeps for iteration, the full sweep before delivery. Thin curled tips split under 1-px erosion — keep tip undersides blunt.
- New font isolated behind its own flag/registry entry so other fonts stay byte-identical (check with a library md5 gate after every change; in the G-type repo that gate is the archive suite, section 19).
- Integration into a product mirrors the previous font's integration point by point (grep its id across the tree: registry, metadata/tags, family/VF builders, OT config, kerning hooks, proofs, stress lanes, deco lane, golden hashes, hard-coded font counts in tests, recipe doc). A font with its own weight ladder needs its own weight-name mapping.
- New font sliders: grep the global PARAMS first (jit, lean, seed, rough, ov, sb are taken) and prefix new keys; generators emit ink-width glyphs at x=0 and let the host own sidebearings; stress every new slider at host level (through the safety net), not only in the standalone generator.
- A long library name can break the cover cap-height test — use a per-font cover tightening flag; a new font changes e2e assumptions (new-sort order, library counts, years); add it to the fitting library collections.
- Porting a Python-designed font to the product generator: prove parity per glyph by XOR (200 px, supersampled, bbox aligned; ≤ 2 %) and reproduce the reference's measurement raster for spacing/kerning. Collections have a size cap — decide which font gives way.
- Stress tests assert absolute properties (counter size, hole count vs the capital, no near-touch), over random parameter mixes, not only equality with the default build.
- Reference books with scans only: read the OCR text layer per page for theory; for specimens render each spread enlarged (~1600 px wide, 2×2 views) in a browser and survey visually; record genre, construction method, contrast/axis, serifs/terminals, proportions and Cyrillic solutions per face.

## 18a. Experimental (non-classical) fonts: explicit allow-list
- The user may ask for fonts that deliberately break classic rules (stains, drips, distortion, fused letters). Allowed within reason: list every relaxed rule explicitly as a per-font allow-list (which glyphs may close counters, from which weight, which gaps may become slits, negative sidebearings, no kerning table) and keep every other rule.
- Never relaxed: misreads (a glyph must not read as another letter, a diacritic or punctuation), stray specks, random topology changes not driven by a parameter, broken export/metadata. The only misread exceptions are look-alike twins that the allow-list names and the UI discloses (Сцепка О = 0: plaque «Могут путаться: буква «О» и цифра «0»»); a shared Cyrillic/Latin glyph is not an exception.
- Deformation follows a parameter (weight, melt, drip) monotonically: lighter = more readable, heavier = more expressive; key sheet per weight before the full set.
- Six historical ways to make a display/«alternative» face (Карпенко): unusual reproduction technology; imitation of another technology (stencil, typewriter, pixel, LED); historic stylisation; distortion of a normal face (shape, proportion, broken baseline, «print-defect» texture); calligraphic elements in type; merging 2+ letterforms. Name the method in the brief — it tells which rules may bend. Mapping in G-type (`kb/TYPO_KARPENKO_KB.md`): Капля = 4, Пиксель = 2, Модуль = 1/6, Рывок = 4/6, Устав and Ротунда = 3 + 5.
- Relaxations live in the font's own proof/stress hooks, never in global checks; the font is tagged «Экспериментальные». The heat map and the proof word intended irregularity as «Неровность задумана рисунком шрифта» for Модуль, Рывок, Тяга and for a user font with the «Неровность задумана» switch, and offer no «Исправить» there (11 Nov 2026).
- Complex organic letters: build from a field/skeleton, not hand-drawn outlines; seed randomness by glyph id so builds are stable; keep a safe fallback rebuild (less deformation) that kicks in automatically when topology differs from a clean reference.
- Allow-lists in force (glyphs · condition · source):
  - Капля: counters close only in `F.allowClose` (a e е ё з б в д р ф Ф ы ь э ю я Ж З 3 6 8 9 0 & № …) and only at melt ≥ 60 or stem ≥ 150; О Н П stay open; new pockets never; channels (closing gap ≤ 200 px at 4 u) tolerated at stem ≥ 150 or melt ≥ 70; islands < 420 u² and holes with inscribed r < 16 u are removed; negative sidebearings at Black are by design; no kerning table; drips only where ink meets the baseline, satellites only directly under their own bead (stress error `misread`); proof counts parts minus satellites (RECIPE_kaplya).
  - Аппликация: pieces overlap (parts = reference); gaps > 0 split only the end pieces flagged `gl` (`F.proof.drops`); crooked, uneven shapes and scatter by design; excluded from outline hygiene (it stays crooked).
  - Сцепка: О = 0 twins; Bold/Regular stem 1.10; ligature glyphs are not kerned against neighbours (outline carries baked sidebearings); every gap ≥ g or 0.
  - Модуль, Рывок, Тяга: intended irregular rhythm; Модуль and Рывок have no overshoot (flat, squarish rounds), Рывок horns hang into the neighbour space (proof hook `hang` skips «за поля» / «тесные поля» for it only; `stemFy` samples stems at 0.62–0.68 h; real check = no ink overlap in the four test words); Тяга: exact geometry (`HYG.tyaga`: no snapping, no blunting), Bold/Regular ≈ 1.13.
  - Кортес: stems 0.98 S (±3 %) with deliberately thinner N И M (0.8 S), U right stem 0.72 S, Ж centre 0.70 S; И/N is an inherent mirror pair; the node budget is ~75; swash tails only by the pair rule (section 18b).
  - Нить: pen dabs (dots, ticks) are the only intended ink islands; no node cap.
  - Кодекс: flat pen (axis ≈ 0°) and the uncial round Е are style decisions; Е must still differ from Є at 12 px.
  - Столб: counters as narrow as cx = max(22, .26 S) by the poster reference; shared glyphs Н/H and п/n are exempt from the misread audit by project rule.

## 18b. Per-typeface decisions (owner-approved unless marked (recipe) or (audit); one bullet per decision)
Sources: the owner's remarks in the Claude chat and memory (25 Oct – 12 Nov 2026), `service/RECIPE_*.md`, `service/FIX_*.md`, `kb/AUDIT_m29.md`. Construction numbers live in section 11; this section keeps what the owner decided.
- **Owner baseline for every font**: clean, straight, even shapes (no crooked, slanted or strange forms unless irregularity is the font's point); capitals, lowercase and digits consistent inside one font; letters that repeat across Cyrillic and Latin (М/M, м/m …) share one construction in every font; Latin r and n are original English forms, never copies of г/п; a red sketch over our render is followed exactly (not more timid, not «перестарался»), a tick freezes the glyph; a new font is shown for approval before integration; open fonts are study material, never outline bases.
- **Вольт (`volt`) and Квадра (`kvadra`)** (audit; no recipe file; code core11–14): З and 3 are different glyphs in both (m29: identical outlines); accent gap max(40 u, 0.32 stem) + overshoot; Квадра keeps hairlines proportional to the stem (hz/stem ≥ 0.78, monotone: .90/.79/.56/.44 was the defect), near-circle ovals 0.95/0.56; Вольт, Квадра and Кодекс: Latin m arched by default, Cyrillic-style м in ss01 (swapped 15 Nov in `public/patches/latin-m.js`; Кодекс m rebuilt from its own n, the old one had a double-width middle stem); open (FIX_GLYPHS «decision needed»): Вольт sidebearings +4–6 u, weight-ladder retune of Квадра/Вольт/Линия.
- **Кодекс (`codex`)** (audit): flat pen, axis ≈ 0° and the uncial Е stay (design properties); `{ }` one continuous ribbon capped at 0.9 of the bend radius; & upper loop open with a thinner ribbon at heavy weights; m29 proposals: a longer ъ flag (≥ 1.4 × ь) and a tailed l or slab I against the ь/ъ and I/l look-alikes.
- **Линия (`liniya`)** (audit, FIX_GLYPHS): monoline, contrast .91; tailed l; Black Й/Ă breve capped at 0.62 of the arc depth so the bar never closes it; “ ‘ are the 180° rotation of ” ’; minus on the + axis, „ hangs at the baseline.
- **Пропись (`propis`)** (recipe, SKELETON_API): pointed-pen script; lowercase enters at (0, 0.2 xh) and exits at (w, yc), angle 52°, overlap 4 u; after о в б ю o b v w the next letter takes its `.hi` variant (calt); no kerning across a connection (connected pairs 0); «связность» below 100 % shortens entry/exit; heights must not grow with weight (compensate the pen offset; Й Black 6 u over its zone is open); `uprightAngle` 0, italicAngle measured; VF axis wght only.
- **Ренесса (`renessa`)**: capitals even in weight and colour (no letter heavier from strokes or accents; widths balance to 0.73–1.2 of H/O/N); Z z match the style, corners clean, no diagonal poking past the bar; 7 without overhang; Э = mirrored С + bar, the bar a wavy calligraphic stroke with a small left flick; б flag = light wavy ribbon, swelling then tapering up and right with a slight upturn, weight like the r arm; Ъ left tail long as in the reference; capital Л and Д match (Λ-form in caps only, lowercase classical); ф short ascender with a left head serif; 2 base ends in an upward wedge tick; Ц Щ ц щ tail = baseline bar continuing right, ending in a small flared wedge foot; r arm curls into a downward terminal; every serif, spur, tick and flag is an angular wedge like the font's own (28 Oct 09:02), never straight or round; ( ) are arcs, “ ‘ rotated ” ’; & = one smooth stroke, advance 570.
- **Строка (`stroka`)**: junction style of b d p q h m n approved — straight stem, the round form branching off with a smooth cleft, applied to every Cyrillic and Latin letter, classical letter forms; the «б-cap» (round form rises, one wide corner, short horizontal arm with a slanted cut; flipped for bottoms, mirrored for left ends) is the signature terminal for all caps and tails, Latin included (a c e s t l J j g y f r 2 3 5 6 9 С З Э Є); never cut a round form on its descending shoulder; squircle ovals without stadium ends; hair/stem .94/.89/.85/.75 (.80 closed e/a counters at Black); Ж К ink-trap slot with a rounded tip, diagonals thinned (К .96, Ж .90); R Я leg one constant-width diagonal; З clean wedge notch, round top vs flat-top 3; 3 smaller upper half; 4 flush bar; б bowl fully rounded like 6; ф ю built from b/d bowls with thin crotches, ф ×1.14; Л Д vertical legs with a rounded foot; Ц Щ short square tail; m ≈ 1.5 n with equal clefts; u = n rotated; l a short curved tail (t l J one hook family); Latin m arched by default, ss01 = м; italic = compensated slant, no cursive forms.
- **Устав (`ustav`)** (recipe, audit): classified «Древнерусские · Устав», never «Вязь»; unicase; У with a straight diagonal arm (a curved one read as Ч); Е/F with a short tick ≤ 0.05 h; Latin l tailed; breve middle ≥ 0.35 stem; comma tail ≤ −150 u (the old −333…−457 pushed the line to 1.52 em).
- **Ротунда (`rotunda`)**: Е = stem + bars (the round form read as Є); М straight stems + wedge to the baseline; 1 with a low flat flag; Й breve = small nib-arc cup; & = open upper bowl + open lower arc + leg; ? = clean hook ending above the dot; № = N + ring 0.22 h + underline; « » big chevrons; flat-top 3 vs З; nib compensated per weight (39/35/30/28°) so the axis stays 30–32°; rejected on this engine: two-arc lancet bowls, diagonal foot strokes (a real foot needs a terminal primitive).
- **Модуль (`modul`)** (rule of 26 Oct): every glyph is built from lines and has at least one rounding AND at least one hole of the keyhole kind; reference letters Ж А Ш О Н; punctuation only needs rounding; round forms use keyholes, diagonal letters (К X V Л N) use bands and wedge pockets, otherwise they read as H/U/П; А = arch with a closed head + bar + slot open downward; Л = Λ of two bands; З/3 two heads + a right waist notch; Y/У band fork + post; W two V bands in one contour; М walls + V band with the notch vertex at 0.5 h; G aperture on top + shelf; stems with keyholes ≤ +8 % of the normal stem.
- **Рывок (`ryvok`)** (recipe, audit): horns grow out of the stroke along the baseline and bars, 60–70 % of letters carry one, rounds О 0 8 Ф Q and V W Y У carry none; tip 28–34 u; rejected: uniform ears, thin spurs under the baseline; word space ≥ 330, spacing and kerning from the body band, horn-to-horn clearance ≥ 0.06 capH; Ё/ё dots clear of the horn (gap ≥ 0.3 stem); m29 finding: Э looked like a rotated Ɔ with a bar.
- **Капля (`kaplya`)** (25–26 Oct): one design whose weights spread — heavier = more deformed and tighter, Black = one stain, Light stays readable; true lowercase, Latin + Cyrillic basic set; presets Капля, Чистая, Пятно, Дождь; 6 ≠ 0 at 12–24 px; allow-list in 18a.
- **Пиксель (`pixel`)** (26 Oct, full charset): resolution 6–24 cells per cap, weight by cell count, grid shape (square, dot, rounded, diamond, gap, stagger), «пиксельная кисть» / «стёрка» edit the font's own grid per resolution; wants a VF (wght only); 0 slashed by default, ss01 = Cyrillic-style m + plain zero; Ш Щ tail 2 cells deep with a left foot bar; Latin m arched by default (15 Nov).
- **Аппликация (`applik`)** (29 Oct, basic charset): child-like cut paper, every letter unlike the others; main look = clean scissors, «Рваный край» an adjustable level (default 0, not a second font); gaps adjustable (default glued); font-specific sliders that follow its construction; Ш Щ Ц base bar never overhangs the stems (lead fix); lowercase height 0.86 capH.
- **Сцепка (`stsepka`)** (29–30 Oct, basic charset): one corner radius for all glyphs like О; Н+И, М+Е, С+Т, П+Н must not merge (the Latin pair HA and the МИН triple were dropped later in P1); favourite interlocks Г+О, Р+О, Г+А («roof» devices); К and У as sketched by the owner (К stem + short stub + near-vertical arms; У = Ч-top + U-cup with one slit); Й as variant A (solid triangle fused to the stem and the cap line, one wide slit); Ж in one contour (no separate parts); Ц Щ tails as thick as the bottom bar; Л legs of equal thickness (the left was thicker); А and О narrower; strict construction grid, optically even «blocky» spacing, one slit g = inter-letter gap = roof slit; default calt = safe caps set, ss02 dense + lowercase, ss01 off.
- **Столб (`stolb`)** (1 Nov, basic charset; narrow, extended): А = Л + a centre bar; equal counter width inside every glyph; diagonals optically 1.03 × the stem (Л У Х К looked thinner than verticals), И/N/Й diagonal one straight stroke with parallel edges between the inner corners; Д/д no descender feet — a solid base bar at the baseline, 0.25 S wider per side; Ф/ф a through stem in the middle of the counter, two equal narrow counters; closed-aperture terminals С С с G Э З S 5 6 9 end at one small gap; 3 З з В with a V-notch on the right at the waist (depth 0.35 S); Ж = three full-height stems with arrow connectors + outer V-notches; Э = mirrored С + short tongue; М two stems + a narrow centre V; Х two stems + bowtie connector; У variants A/B/C (default A, lowercase у = B); G closed С with a lower stem to 36 % h; З/3/5/С/Э/S bottom-left rounding equals top-left; Latin m arched, Cyrillic м a wedge; «Афиша» is only a preset (stem 124, wdth 92, xh 540, urnd 40, uctr 20, uap 90, ugap 22).

### Knowledge bases: `.cursor/skills/type-design-foundation/kb/`
Read the file the task needs, grep the large ones, never load them whole. Section 16 is the digest of the ParaType part.
- `kb/PARATYPE_KB.md` (10 KB) — condensed ParaType glossary: classification, anatomy RU ↔ EN, metrics and units, charset and typesetting rules, technology. Read first for any tag, UI label, brief or report wording.
- `kb/paratype_glossary.md` (70 KB) — structured copy of all 454 terms by letter; only 89 have definitions (up to «Гротески, новые»), the rest are term-only. Grep a term when PARATYPE_KB is not enough.

- `kb/TYPO_BANNIKOVA_KB.md` (5 KB) — Bannikova: classification by skeleton, width variation and rhythm, the «частокол», Байконур small-size rules. Read before drawing or judging a text antiqua or Cyrillic rhythm.

- `kb/AFISHA_LESSONS_v2.md` (2 KB) — Афиша 2 (stopped): approved and rejected forms, method lessons. Read before serif display work and whenever a whole terminal family gets crossed.
- `kb/AUDIT_m29.md` (14 KB) — audit of nine fonts, 26 Oct 2026: measured table, 20 findings, resolution. Read when auditing a font or setting a numeric threshold (accent gap, Bold ratio, word space, punctuation scale).
